# Export a customer's data or delete their account

Customers can ask to see the data you hold about them, or to have it removed. You do both from the customer's page.

## Before you start

- Exporting needs the switch "Export everything held about a customer (a personal data access request) as a zip file". Admins always have it. A moderator needs the owner to switch it on.
- Deleting a customer who has history needs permission to anonymise customers, cancel orders and adjust wallets. Admins have all three.
- Deleting cannot be undone. Read this guide first.

## Export a customer's personal data

1. Open the customer's page.
2. Click **More actions**, then **Export personal data**.
3. Click **Download the zip**.

![The export message](/screenshots/customer-export.png)

The file is named `customer-{id}-personal-data` with the date. It contains the customer's details, orders, tickets, payments, wallet history, withdrawals, prize claims, messages and consent records. It leaves out their password, the full bank account number (only the name and last four digits are included), other people's details, and your staff notes and security checks.

Every export is recorded against your name.

## Delete a customer

Open the customer's page, click **More actions**, then **Delete customer**. What happens depends on whether they have any history.

![The Delete customer message](/screenshots/customer-delete.png)

### A customer with no history

If they have no orders, wallet entries or withdrawals, the account is deleted completely. Click **Yes, delete**.

### A customer with history

The message is called **Delete this customer and clear everything**. It will:

- Cancel and refund their live orders, and take their tickets out of the draws.
- Remove their Site Credit.
- Remove their name, email, phone, address, date of birth and bank details, and close the account.
- Keep their orders, tickets and wallet history in your records, under "Deleted customer #ID".

If they have money in their **Cash Wallet**, a red warning shows how much, and a tick box says "I understand £X of the customer's money will be removed and NOT paid to them". Customers should be paid what they are owed, so pay it out with a withdrawal first if you can. If you carry on, the amount is recorded in the wallet history against your name.

Click **Yes, delete and clear everything**.

### When it is blocked

The message says **This customer cannot be deleted yet** and lists what to deal with first. It is blocked when the customer has:

- A withdrawal that is not finished.
- An order in progress (within 3 hours).
- A prize claim that has not been fulfilled.
- Held Cash Wallet money.
- An order that is still being given its tickets.
- An order that also entered a competition that has already been drawn.

## When customers do it themselves

Customers do not have to ask you. On **Account**, **Account Details**, a **Download your data** box gives them a zip of everything we hold about them, and a **Delete your account** box removes their personal details. Both ask for their password again, and deleting also asks them to type DELETE.

A customer can delete their own account only when nothing is outstanding. If something is, they are told exactly what, in plain words:

- Money in their Cash Wallet (they should withdraw it first) or Site Credit (they spend it, or ask you to remove it).
- A withdrawal, order or prize that has not finished.
- Tickets in a competition that has not been drawn yet.
- A self-excluded or closed account. These cannot be deleted by the customer, so the protection stays in place. Contact them after the exclusion has ended if they still want it removed.

Each download they make is recorded in the same list as yours, with the customer as the person who exported it. When a customer deletes their account they are emailed a record of it (the **Account Deleted** email under **Email**, **Notifications**), and everything is handled exactly like a deletion you do yourself: their records are kept without their personal details.

## What your customers will see

A deleted customer can no longer sign in. Their records are kept for your accounts, under the "Deleted customer" name.

## Tips

- Export the data first if you are not sure what to keep.
- Pay out any Cash Wallet balance before deleting, if the customer is owed it.
- Keep a record of why you deleted an account.

## Common questions

**What is the difference between deleting and anonymising?**
There is no separate anonymise button. Deleting a customer with history anonymises them and keeps the records. Deleting one with no history removes them completely.

**Can a moderator delete a customer?**
Only if the owner has given them the Customers area, and for a customer with history the anonymise, cancel and wallet permissions as well. Only give moderators the Customers area if you trust them with it.
