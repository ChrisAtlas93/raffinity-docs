# Find and manage customers

The **Customers** screen lists everyone with an account on your site. From it you can find a customer, see their orders and tickets, add someone by hand, ban an account and more.

## Before you start

- **Customers** is open to admins. A moderator can only open it if the owner has switched on "Open customer accounts (names, contact details and history)", which is off by default.
- Only Raffinity can add admins and moderators. Ask Raffinity to add a new team member.

## Find a customer

1. Open **Customers**, then **Customers**.
2. Use the tabs: **Customers**, **Staff** and **All accounts**. Each shows a count.
3. Use **Search** for a name, email, phone number or ID.
4. Open **Filters** to narrow by when they registered, email verified or not, whether their personal data has been removed, whether they have paid orders, account restrictions, flags, how much they have spent, and when they last paid.

![The Customers list](/screenshots/customers-list.png)

The cards at the top show **Total customers**, **Registered in last 30 days**, **With a paid order** and **Active bans**. Each row shows the **Customer**, their **Account status**, **Orders**, **Total spent**, **Last paid order** and when they **Registered**.

## Read a customer's page

Click a customer to open their page. It shows:

- Cards for **Total orders**, **Total spent** (paid orders only), **Active tickets** and **Instant wins**.
- Tabs for their **Orders**, **Tickets** and **Instant wins**. The orders tab lets you search and filter by date and payment status.
- **Customer details**: name, email, phone, date of birth, when they registered, when their email was verified and their role.
- **Billing address**, with a **Copy** button.
- Notices at the top if the account is anonymised, banned, has withdrawals frozen or is flagged.

![A customer's page](/screenshots/customer-page.png)

## Add a customer by hand

1. On the **Customers** list, click **Add user**. Only admins see this button.
2. Fill in the form. For a customer, choose the **Customer** role and add their name, email, phone number and date of birth. Customers must be 18 or over.
3. You can mark **Email address already confirmed**.
4. Leave **Email them a link to choose their own password** on. It is recommended, so nobody ever has to be told a password. If you turn it off, you set a password yourself.
5. Click to add them. You will see "User added".

![The Add a user form](/screenshots/customer-add-user.png)

## Ban or unban a customer

1. Open the customer's page and click **Manage role / ban**.
2. Switch **Banned** on or off and type a **Reason**.
3. Click **Save changes**.

![Manage role and ban](/screenshots/customer-manage-role.png)

A banned customer cannot sign in, and their sessions are ended. They see that their account has been suspended and to contact support. Because they cannot sign in, they also cannot withdraw. This is different from a closed or self-excluded account, where withdrawals still work.

Only Raffinity can change someone's role. The **Role** box is greyed out for other staff.

## Verify an email by hand

If a customer cannot verify their email, open their page, click **More actions**, then **Verify email**. This only shows when the email is not yet verified.

## What your customers will see

Nothing changes on their side unless you ban them, in which case they cannot sign in.

## Tips

- Use the **Customers** tab to leave staff out of your search.
- Check a customer's **Orders** tab before you answer a complaint.
- Write a clear reason when you ban someone.

## Common questions

**Can I see a customer's password?**
No. Nobody can. Send them a reset link instead.

**How do I add a moderator or an admin?**
Ask Raffinity.

**How do I find customers who have spent a lot?**
Use the **Total spent** filter.
