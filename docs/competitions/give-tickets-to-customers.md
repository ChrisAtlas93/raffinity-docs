# Give tickets to customers

Ticket Allocation lets you give tickets to one or more customers without them paying. Use it for postal entries, for tickets you are giving as a prize, or as a goodwill gesture. Only admins can use it.

## Before you start

- You need to be an admin.
- Know the email address each customer used to register.
- Know which competition and how many tickets for each.

## Give the tickets

1. Open **Competitions**, then **Ticket Allocation**.
2. Under **Customers**, type the **Customer Email(s)**. Add one email per line, or separate several with commas.
3. Under **Prizes**, choose the **Product**. This is the competition. The list shows your active competitions.
4. Set the **Ticket Quantity**.
5. To give tickets for more than one competition, click **Add another prize** and fill in another row.
6. Click **Assign Tickets**.

![The Ticket Allocation screen](/screenshots/ticket-allocation.png)

## What happens

A free order is created for each customer, so the tickets show in their account like any others. The usual limits still apply. A customer cannot be given more than the **Max tickets per person**, and you cannot give more tickets than are left. If the competition has an entry question, it is treated as answered correctly.

## Check what was assigned

Below the form is a history table. For each batch it shows the **Order #**, the customer, the products, how many tickets were requested and allocated, and a status: Pending, Allocating, Allocated, Partial or Failed. Click **View Order** to open an order.

## What your customers will see

The tickets appear in their account and in their order history. They are in the draw like any other ticket.

## Tips

- Check the number of tickets left on the competition before you assign.
- If the status is **Partial** or **Failed**, open the order to see what happened, and try again for the missing tickets.
- Keep a note of why you gave the tickets.

## Common questions

**Why does the form say Prize and Product?**
Those labels mean the competition you are giving tickets for.

**Can a moderator use this?**
No. It is for admins only.

**Does this count towards revenue?**
The order is free, so no money is taken.
