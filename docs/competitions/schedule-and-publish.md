# Schedule and publish a competition

The **Schedule & Status** panel decides when a competition is on sale and when it ends. This guide shows how to publish now, how to schedule it for later, and how a competition closes.

## Before you start

- Create your competition. See [Create a competition](/competitions/create-a-competition).
- Wait until its tickets have been prepared. A competition cannot go on sale before that. See [Why a competition says it is preparing tickets](/competitions/tickets-being-prepared).

## Set the dates

In the **Schedule & Status** panel on the right of the form, fill in:

- **Start time.** When ticket sales begin. Required.
- **End time.** When ticket sales end. Required. It must be after the start time.
- **Draw date.** When you will draw the winner. Required. It can be the same as the end time.

![The Schedule & Status panel](/screenshots/schedule-and-status.png)

## Choose a status

The **Status** has four choices.

- **Draft.** Saved but not visible to customers. New competitions start here.
- **Active.** On sale.
- **Closed.** Ticket sales have stopped and the competition is waiting for its draw.
- **Completed.** The draw has been made.

## Publish now

1. Set **Status** to **Active**.
2. Click **Save changes**.

## Publish later

You have two choices.

- **Scheduled publish.** Pick a time in this box. The competition moves from Draft to Active by itself at that time. This is the only thing that publishes a draft for you. A draft that only has a future **Start time** stays a draft until you set it to **Active** yourself.
- **Active with a future start time.** Customers can see the competition and a "Starting soon" countdown, but they cannot buy until the start time. Your site opens it for sale at that moment.

When you pick a future start time, the form may set the status back to Draft. If you want the countdown to show, change **Status** to **Active** after you choose the time.

## Let it close and draw by itself

Switch on **Enable autodraw** if you want the winner picked automatically as soon as the competition closes. A competition closes by itself at the end time, or as soon as it sells out. See [Draws and winners](/draws-and-winners/).

To stop sales sooner, open the **More actions** menu and choose **Close Now**. Only admins can do this, unless the owner has switched it on for moderators.

## What your customers will see

- Active competitions can be bought.
- A competition that has not started shows "Starting soon" with a countdown.
- A closed competition shows that sales are closed and the draw is awaited.

## Tips

- Check the end time carefully. Sales stop exactly then.
- Look at the competition on your live site after you publish it.

## Common questions

**Why can customers not buy my active competition?**
Its start time may be in the future, or its tickets may still be being prepared.

**What does Needs attention mean in the list?**
A competition that is still open after its end time, or whose draw date has passed with no winner. See [Find and manage your competitions](/competitions/find-and-manage-competitions).
