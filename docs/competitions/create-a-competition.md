# Create a competition

A competition is what your customers buy tickets for. You set it up on one form with five tabs. This guide takes you through the form and tells you which fields you must fill in.

## Before you start

- You need to be an admin. A moderator can only do this if the owner has switched on "Create and edit competitions, categories and instant-win prizes".
- Have the prize details, the ticket price, how many tickets you will sell and a picture ready.
- Decide your start, end and draw dates.

## Open the form

1. In the admin menu, open **Competitions**, then **Competitions**.
2. Click **New competition**.

You will see the page **Create Competition** with five tabs: **Details**, **Tickets & pricing**, **Instant wins**, **Games & engagement** and **Display & promotion**. A red star beside a label means you must fill it in.

![The Create Competition form](/screenshots/new-competition.png)

## Fill in the Details tab

1. Type the **Name**. This is the title customers see.
2. Type a **Description**.
3. If you want an entry question, add it now. See [Add an entry question](/competitions/add-an-entry-question).
4. Under **Winners**, check **Number of winners**. It starts at 1, which is right for a single prize. A higher number draws that many different tickets. The switch **A customer can win more than once** controls whether one customer can take several places.

## Fill in the Tickets & pricing tab

Set the **Ticket price**, **Total tickets** and **Max tickets per person**. These three are required. See [Set the ticket price, number of tickets and limits](/competitions/set-ticket-price-and-limits).

## Fill in the side panel

On the right you will find **Schedule & Status**, **Images** and **Categories**.

- Set the **Start time**, **End time** and **Draw date**. All three are required. See [Schedule and publish a competition](/competitions/schedule-and-publish).
- Add a **Featured image**. See [Add pictures and categories](/competitions/add-pictures-and-categories).

## Save it

1. Click **Create** at the top of the page.
2. Your site starts making the ticket numbers. See [Why a competition says it is preparing tickets](/competitions/tickets-being-prepared).

New competitions are saved as **Draft**, so customers cannot see them yet.

If the form cannot be saved, the tab with the problem shows a red number. Open it and fix the fields marked in red.

## What your customers will see

Nothing yet. Customers only see a competition once it is **Active**.

## Tips

- Save as a draft first and check everything before you go live.
- Use **Duplicate** to copy a competition you have already set up. See [Duplicate a competition](/competitions/duplicate-a-competition).
- The **Instant wins**, **Games & engagement** and **Display & promotion** tabs are optional. See [Instant wins and games](/instant-wins-and-games/).

## Common questions

**Can I change things after I create the competition?**
Yes. Open it from the list and click **Save changes** when you finish. Some things are limited once tickets have sold. For example, the total number of tickets can only go up.

**Do I have to fill in every tab?**
No. Only the fields marked with a red star are required.
