# Add pictures and categories

Good pictures help a competition sell. Categories help customers find it. Both are set in the side panel of the competition form.

## Before you start

- Have your pictures ready, or upload them to the **Media** library first.
- Create your categories if you need new ones. See [Manage your categories](/competitions/manage-categories).

## Add a featured image

1. Open your competition.
2. In the **Images** panel, click **Choose featured image**.
3. Pick a picture from your library, or upload a new one.
4. Click **Save changes**.

The featured image is the main picture for the competition on your site.

## Add gallery pictures

1. In the **Images** panel, click **Add gallery images**.
2. Pick one or more pictures.
3. Drag the pictures into the order you want. They are shown in that order.

![The Images and Categories panels](/screenshots/images-and-categories.png)

## Choose categories

1. In the **Categories** panel, click **Select an option**.
2. Choose a category. You can choose more than one.
3. Need a new category? Click the **+** button beside the box. You will be asked for a **Name**, **Slug** and **Sort order**.
4. Click **Save changes**.

## What your customers will see

The featured image is the competition's main picture on your home page and on the competition page. Gallery pictures appear on the competition page. Categories appear as tabs on your home page, so customers can filter the competitions.

## Tips

- Use clear, bright pictures of the actual prize.
- Use the same style and shape for every competition so your site looks neat.
- Check how the picture looks on a phone.

## Common questions

**Where do my pictures live?**
In the **Media** item at the top of your admin menu.

**Can one competition be in several categories?**
Yes.
