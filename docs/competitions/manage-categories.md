# Manage your categories

Categories group your competitions, for example Cash, Cars or Holidays. Customers use them to filter the competitions on your home page. You manage them in one place.

## Before you start

- You need to be an admin. A moderator needs the switch for creating and editing competitions, categories and instant-win prizes.

## See your categories

1. In the admin menu, open **Competitions**, then **Categories**.
2. The list shows each category's **Name**, **Slug**, the number of **Competitions** in it and its **Sort order**. Use the **Search** box to find one.

![The Categories list](/screenshots/categories-list.png)

## Add a category

1. Click **New category**.
2. Type a **Name**. Required.
3. Type a **Slug**. Required. This is the short version used in the web address, for example `watches-and-jewellery`.
4. Set the **Sort order**. Lower numbers appear first.
5. Click **Create**. Or click **Create & create another** to add more.

![The Create Category form](/screenshots/category-create.png)

## Change or reorder a category

- Click **Edit** beside a category to rename it or change its sort order.
- To reorder by dragging, click the reorder icon at the top left of the list, then drag the rows.

## Delete a category

You can only delete a category that is empty. Check the **Competitions** column. If the number is above 0, the **Delete** button is greyed out, and pointing at it tells you how many competitions are still in the category.

To empty a category, open each competition in it and change its **Categories** (see [Add pictures and categories](/competitions/add-pictures-and-categories)). When the count reaches 0, click **Delete** and confirm.

## What your customers will see

Categories appear as tabs on your home page, in your sort order. Customers click one to see only those competitions.

## Tips

- Keep the list short. A handful of clear categories works better than many.
- A category with no live competitions has nothing to show, so think about whether it is needed.
- You can also delete a category from its edit page. The same rule applies.

## Common questions

**Can I add a category while I am creating a competition?**
Yes. Use the **+** button beside the **Categories** box. See [Add pictures and categories](/competitions/add-pictures-and-categories).

**Can a competition be in more than one category?**
Yes.
