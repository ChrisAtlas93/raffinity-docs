# Add an entry question

An entry question is a question customers answer before they can add tickets to their basket. You choose the question, up to three answers and the correct one. A separate setting decides what happens when someone answers wrongly. Read the section on that setting carefully.

## Before you start

- Write a clear question with one correct answer.
- Decide whether wrong answers should still get tickets. See [Decide what happens to wrong answers](#decide-what-happens-to-wrong-answers).

## Add the question

1. Open your competition and stay on the **Details** tab.
2. Find the **Entry Question** section.
3. Type your **Question**.
4. Type up to three answers in **Answer 1**, **Answer 2** and **Answer 3**.
5. Choose the **Correct answer**.
6. Click **Save changes**.

To have no question, leave the **Question** box empty.

![The Entry Question section](/screenshots/entry-question.png)

## Decide what happens to wrong answers

This is a setting for your whole site, not for one competition.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Business & Site Settings**.
2. Find the section **Entry Question**.
3. Look at the switch **Allocate tickets for incorrect answers**.

![The Allocate tickets for incorrect answers switch](/screenshots/entry-question-setting.png)

- **On (the default).** A customer who answers wrongly still gets their tickets, and those tickets can win.
- **Off.** A customer who answers wrongly cannot buy. They are told the answer is incorrect and asked to try again.

Which is right for you is your decision. If you are unsure how this fits with your terms and conditions, ask your legal adviser.

## What your customers will see

They see the question on the competition page and must choose an answer before adding tickets to their basket. With the switch off, a wrong answer stops them buying and they see "Incorrect answer", with a prompt to try again.

## Tips

- Keep the question easy. It should not stop honest customers from entering.
- Check your question wording on your live site on a phone.
- Your site wording for the skill question is in **Website**, then **Site Wording**.

## Common questions

**Is the customer's answer recorded?**
Whether they answered correctly is saved with the order.

**Can I use a different question for each competition?**
Yes. The question is set on each competition. The wrong-answer switch applies to all of them.
