# Delete a competition

You can only delete a competition that has never had an order. After the first order, a competition must stay on record, because deleting it would also delete the customers' orders and tickets. Close it instead.

## Before you start

- Open **Competitions**, then **Competitions**.
- Check whether anyone has bought tickets. The **Sold** column in the list shows this.

## Delete a competition with no orders

1. Open the competition from the list, then click **More actions**.
2. Click **Delete** and confirm.

You can also use the row menu in the list, where **Delete** appears for competitions that have never had an order.

## If tickets have sold

**Delete** is not available. If you try, you are told the competition has had orders and to close it instead.

To stop sales, open the competition, click **More actions**, then **Close Now**. Only admins can do this, unless the owner has switched it on for moderators. A closed competition stays on record and can still be drawn.

If a competition must be cancelled for good, you refund the orders one by one. See [Orders and refunds](/orders-and-refunds/).

## What your customers will see

A deleted competition disappears from your site. A closed competition shows that sales are closed.

## Tips

- Think carefully before you close a competition early. Customers have paid for tickets.

## Common questions

**Can I delete several competitions at once?**
Only super admins can bulk delete. Competitions that have had orders are kept.

**Can I get a deleted competition back?**
No. Deleting is permanent.
