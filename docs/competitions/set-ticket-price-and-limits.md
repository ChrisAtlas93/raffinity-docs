# Set the ticket price, number of tickets and limits

The **Tickets & pricing** tab controls what a ticket costs and how many can be bought. These settings protect your margins and keep entry fair. Get them right before you put a competition on sale.

## Before you start

- Work out your prize cost, so you can set a price that covers it.
- Decide how many tickets you want to sell and how many one customer may hold.

## Set the basics

1. Open your competition and click the **Tickets & pricing** tab.
2. Under **Ticket Configuration**, fill in:
   - **Ticket price**. In pounds. Required.
   - **Total tickets**. How many you will sell. Required.
   - **Max tickets per person**. The most one customer can hold. Required.
3. Click **Save changes** (or **Create**, if the competition is new).

![The Tickets & pricing tab](/screenshots/tickets-and-pricing-tab.png)

## The other fields

- **Cash alternative value.** The cash amount you are offering instead of the prize. It is shown to customers on the competition page. The platform does not pay it out for you, so you arrange it with the winner yourself.
- **Prize cost.** What the prize costs you. This is optional and never shown on your site. The reporting dashboard uses it to show your profit.
- **Exclude from Site Credit.** Switch this on if customers must not pay for this competition with Site Credit.

## Know the rules for Total tickets

- You cannot set it lower than the number already sold.
- You cannot set it lower than the highest winning ticket number of any instant win.
- Once anything has sold, you can only raise it, not lower it.
- You cannot lower it while the tickets are still being prepared.

## Know how Max tickets per person is counted

It counts the tickets a customer already holds, plus tickets in orders they have started but not yet paid for. A customer who has reached the limit cannot add more.

## Use Performance Limits for very large competitions

Open the **Performance Limits** section to control how fast tickets can be bought.

- **Use performance limits.** Switched on for you automatically once a competition has 100,000 or more tickets.
- **Max tickets per order.** Leave it blank for the automatic default. The highest you can set is 15,000.
- **Order cooldown (seconds).** How long a customer must wait between orders.

![The Performance Limits section](/screenshots/performance-limits.png)

## What your customers will see

They see the ticket price and how many tickets are left. If they try to buy more than the limit, they are told they have reached it.

## Tips

- Sales, Early Bird and Multibuy prices are set on this same tab. See [Offers and promotions](/offers-and-promotions/).
- Changing the price later does not change what customers have already paid.

## Common questions

**Can I raise the number of tickets after launch?**
Yes. Raise **Total tickets** and save. More ticket numbers are made for you.

**Why can I not lower the total?**
Because tickets have sold, or because a winning instant win ticket number is higher than your new total.
