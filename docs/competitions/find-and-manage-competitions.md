# Find and manage your competitions

The **Competitions** list is the home of all your competitions. Its tabs show you the ones that need attention, and each row has quick actions. This guide shows you around.

## Before you start

- Open **Competitions**, then **Competitions** in the admin menu.

## Use the tabs

![The Competitions list](/screenshots/competitions-list.png)

Each tab shows a number where there is something in it.

- **All.** Every competition.
- **Live now.** On sale at the moment.
- **Ending soon.** Ending within 48 hours.
- **Needs attention.** Still open after its end time, or its draw date has passed with no winner.
- **Awaiting draw.** Closed and waiting for the winner to be drawn.
- **Drafts.** Not yet on sale.
- **Completed.** The draw has been made.

## Read each row

Each row shows the picture and **Name**, how many tickets are **Sold** with a progress bar, the **Price**, whether it is in the **Banner** or has a **Spotlight**, and its **Status**. Small notes under the name show what the competition has, such as instant wins, a mini game, Collect Sets or Early Bird. Notes under the status show things like "Ends in 2 days", "Draw overdue" or "Awaiting draw".

## Search and filter

- Use the **Search** box to find a competition by name.
- Click the funnel icon for **Filters**. You can filter by status, category, banner, spotlight, autodraw, instant wins, mini game, Collect Sets, Early Bird, almost sold out (80% or more) and ending within 7 days.

## Do things to one competition

On the right of each row:

- The eye icon is **Quick look**. It opens a panel with the main details and an **Open competition** button.
- The pencil icon is **Edit**.
- The three dots open a menu: **View on website**, **Copy link**, **Export tickets**, **Duplicate** and, for live competitions, **Close now**. **Delete** appears for competitions that have never had an order.

![The row menu](/screenshots/competition-row-menu.png)

**Export tickets** includes customer email addresses, so only admins see it.

## Do things to several competitions

Tick the boxes beside competitions, then use the bulk actions menu to add them to or remove them from the banner, or to make or remove a spotlight.

## Export the list

Click **Export CSV** at the top to download the list. Only admins see this button.

## What your customers will see

Nothing changes until you edit a competition or change its status.

## Tips

- Check the **Needs attention** tab every day.
- Use **Copy link** to share a competition in an email or on social media.

## Common questions

**Why does a competition say "Draw overdue"?**
It is closed and its draw date has passed with no winner. See [Draws and winners](/draws-and-winners/).

**Why can I not see Close now?**
It only shows for competitions that are active. A moderator also needs permission to close competitions and draw winners.
