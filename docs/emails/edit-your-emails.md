# Edit the emails your customers and staff receive

Your site sends two kinds of email: ones to customers, and alerts to your team. You can switch each on or off, change what it says, and choose who gets the staff alerts. This guide shows how, and lists every email. It is for admins only.

## Before you start

- Set up email sending first. See [Brand your emails and set up sending](/emails/brand-and-send-emails).
- Decide who on your team should receive each alert.

## Choose who gets staff alerts

1. Open **Settings**, then **Email**, then the **Notifications** tab.
2. At the top, under **Who receives admin emails**, add your **Default admin email addresses**. They receive every admin email below.
3. An admin email can have its own addresses in its own section, in **Recipient Email Addresses**. If you leave that empty, the default addresses are used.
4. Click **Save All**.

Some emails need recipients to work. In particular:

- **New Withdrawal Request.** If nobody is listed, nobody is told when a customer asks to withdraw.
- **Complaint Received.** Set this so complaints are not missed.
- **Player Welfare Alert.** Set this so you hear when a player turns red.

## Change an email

1. On the **Notifications** tab, find the email under **Customer Emails** or **Admin Emails**. Click its name to open it.
2. Change what you need:
   - **Enable this email.** An email is only sent while this is on.
   - **Subject Line.**
   - **Email Heading.**
   - **Body.**
   - **Button Text** and **Button URL.**
3. Use the variables shown under the Subject and Body, written in curly brackets, such as `{name}` or `{amount}`. They are replaced with the real details when the email is sent. Each email lists the ones it can use.
4. Click **Save All**.

- New Order
- New Registration
- Instant Win Triggered
- New Withdrawal Request
- Competition Sold Out
- Draw Complete
- Collect the Sets - Set Completed (Admin)
- Contact Form Message
- Stop Marketing Request
- Player Welfare Alert
- Complaint Received

A few staff emails are sent straight away and cannot be changed here. They include the code that unlocks bank details, staff sign-in codes, and payment and ticket alerts.

## What your customers will see

The emails you leave on, in your branding, with your wording.

## Tips

- Keep the legal and safety emails switched on, such as those about limits, breaks and self-exclusion.
- Read each customer email once as a customer would.
- Edit gently. Keep the variables, so the right details still appear.

## Common questions

**Why did a customer not get an email?**
The email may be switched off, or your sending is not set up. Check **Enable this email** and the **Delivery (SMTP)** tab.

**Can I add my own new email?**
No. You can edit and switch off the ones listed here.
