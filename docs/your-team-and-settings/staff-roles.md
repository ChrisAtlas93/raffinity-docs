# Staff roles and who can do what

Everyone who works on your site has a staff account. The role on the account decides what they can see and do. This guide explains the roles, who adds team members, and how you see your team.

## The three roles

- **Admin.** Can use everything described in these guides. Your owner and the people running the business are usually admins.
- **Moderator.** For everyday help. A moderator sees only what an admin has switched on for them. By default a moderator can read contact messages and complaints, edit site wording, mark prizes as sent, and look up orders and tickets. Customers, competitions, promo codes and popups are off by default. Money, reports, settings, payments, wallets, withdrawals, email, branding, ticket allocation and legal pages are never available to a moderator.
- **Super admin.** A role held by the Raffinity team. A few screens are only for them, and they are not covered in these guides.

See [Choose what moderators can do](/your-team-and-settings/moderator-permissions) for the switches.

## See your team

1. Open **Customers**, then **Customers**.
2. Click the **Staff** tab. It lists every admin and moderator, with how many accounts there are.

![The Staff tab](/screenshots/staff-list.png)

**All accounts** shows staff and customers together.

## Add a team member

Only Raffinity can add admins and moderators. Contact Raffinity with the person's name, email address and the role they need. Then:

1. Raffinity creates their account.
2. They can be emailed a link to choose their own password, so nobody ever has to be told a password.
3. They sign in at your site address followed by `/admin`. See [Sign in to your admin](/getting-started/sign-in).

## Change or remove a team member's access

Contact Raffinity to change someone's role. To stop someone signing in straight away, ask Raffinity to ban or remove their account. If a team member leaves, do this the same day.

## What staff can see in the menu

A staff member only sees the menu items they can use. A moderator may see only a handful. If someone says they cannot find a screen, check their role and the switches.

## What your customers will see

Nothing.

## Tips

- Give people the lowest role that lets them do their job.
- Ask Raffinity to remove access as soon as someone leaves.
- Use sign-in codes for extra safety. See [Choose what moderators can do](/your-team-and-settings/moderator-permissions).

## Common questions

**Can I add an admin myself?**
No. Only Raffinity can. This keeps your site secure.

**Can a moderator see money figures?**
No. Moderators never see money figures or reports.

**Can two people share an account?**
Do not. Each person should have their own, so you can see who did what.
