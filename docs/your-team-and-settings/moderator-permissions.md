# Choose what moderators can do and turn on sign-in codes

You decide how much your moderators can do. Everything is off until you switch it on, apart from a few everyday jobs. You can also ask your whole team for an emailed code when they sign in. These settings are for admins only.

## Before you start

- Decide which jobs your moderators should handle, and which only admins should.
- For sign-in codes, your site must be set up to send real emails. See [Brand your emails and set up sending](/emails/brand-and-send-emails).

## Open the settings

Open **Settings**, then **Business & Site Settings**. The two sections you want are **Screens moderators can open** and **What moderators can do**. Click **Save Settings** when you finish.

## Screens moderators can open

These switches decide which screens a moderator can open. Whatever you leave off they cannot even see.

| Switch | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Read and answer contact form messages | On |
| Edit the About page, Contact page, FAQs and site wording, and use the media library | On |
| Mark instant-win and collect-set prizes as claimed, sent or fulfilled | On |
| Look up orders and tickets to help customers (view only) | On |
| Open customer accounts (names, contact details and history) | Off |
| Create and edit competitions, categories and instant-win prizes (this includes prices and limits) | Off |
| Create and edit promo codes | Off |
| Create and edit popups (what visitors see on the website) | Off |

![Screens moderators can open](/screenshots/moderator-screens.png)

Moderators can never open reports and analytics, payments, wallets, withdrawals, settings, email, branding, custom code, ticket allocation or legal pages, whatever you switch on here.

## What moderators can do

These are actions that involve money or personal details. Each is off until you switch it on.

- Add or take money from a customer's wallet, and reverse a top-up.
- Cancel a customer's order and refund it.
- Cancel a customer's order **without** a refund (tickets go back, the money stays paid).
- Refund card payments back to the customer's card (held payments and top-ups).
- Anonymise a customer (remove their personal details, keep their records).
- Export everything held about a customer (a personal data access request) as a zip file.
- Close a competition early and draw its winner.
- Refund an order but keep its tickets in the draw (used after a draw).

![What moderators can do](/screenshots/moderator-actions.png)

These are checked when the action is done, as well as when the button is shown. If a moderator tries something they are not allowed to do, they see "You do not have permission to do this. An admin can allow it in Business & Site Settings."

## Ask your team for a sign-in code

1. In **Business & Site Settings**, find the section **Staff sign-in**.
2. Switch on **Ask team members for an emailed code when they sign in**.
3. Click **Save Settings**.

![The Staff sign-in section](/screenshots/staff-sign-in-setting.png)

It applies to admins, moderators and super admins, on the admin and on your website. It stays off, and cannot be switched on, until your site can send real emails, so nobody can be locked out.

When it is on, a team member signs in as usual, then types a six-digit code we email them. The code lasts 10 minutes. Five wrong tries cancel it, and at most six codes are sent an hour. See [Sign in to your admin](/getting-started/sign-in).

## What your customers will see

Nothing. These settings only affect your team.

## Tips

- Start with the defaults, then add one switch at a time.
- Be careful with money switches. They let moderators move real money.
- Turn on sign-in codes before you take real payments.

## Common questions

**A moderator says they cannot see a screen.**
Check the switch for that screen, and remember some screens are never available to moderators.

**Can I give one moderator more than another?**
No. The switches apply to every moderator.

**What if I lock myself out with sign-in codes?**
Contact Raffinity. They can switch it off for you.
