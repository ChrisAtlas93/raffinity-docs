# A tour of Business & Site Settings

**Business & Site Settings** is one long page of settings for your whole site. This guide lists each section and what it does, and tells you where to read more. It is for admins only.

## Open the page

Open **Settings**, then **Business & Site Settings**. The page is called **Site Settings Page**. Click **Save Settings** when you finish. You will see "Settings saved."

![Business and Site Settings](/screenshots/site-settings-page.png)

## The sections

**Holding Page.** Replaces your site with a maintenance or coming soon page. See [Show a holding page and set your company details](/your-website/holding-page-and-company-details).

**Company & Footer.** Your company name and registration number, shown in your footer. Same guide.

**Announcement bar.** Up to four short lines across the top of your site. Same guide.

**Free Postal Entry Route.** Your postal address and your free entry terms. See [Set up free postal entry](/competitions/free-postal-entry).

**Prize Pack Reveal.** Sets the dividing lines for card rarity in Collect the Sets: **Rare from (£)** and **Legendary from (£)**. See [Run Collect the Sets](/instant-wins-and-games/collect-the-sets).

![Prize Pack Reveal](/screenshots/prize-pack-reveal.png)

**Visit counting and sources.** Whether your site counts visits and remembers where visitors came from, and how it asks about cookies. See [Count visits and connect ad platforms](/marketing-and-tracking/connect-tracking).

**Staff sign-in.** Asks your team for an emailed code when they sign in. See [Choose what moderators can do and turn on sign-in codes](/your-team-and-settings/moderator-permissions).

**Screens moderators can open** and **What moderators can do.** The switches for your moderators. Same guide.

**Entry Question.** The switch **Allocate tickets for incorrect answers**. It is on by default. When it is on, a customer who answers an entry question wrongly still gets tickets, and those tickets can win. When it is off, a wrong answer blocks the purchase. See [Create a competition](/competitions/create-a-competition#add-an-entry-question).

## Other settings pages

Some settings live on their own pages:

- **Settings**, **Payments**: your card providers. See [Set up card payments](/payments/set-up-card-payments).
- **Settings**, **Business Rules**: **Wallet Rules**, **Player Protection** and **Instant Win Settings**. See [Understand the wallet and set your wallet rules](/wallets-and-withdrawals/wallet-rules), [Set when players are flagged](/player-protection/protection-settings) and [Send prizes and show winners](/instant-wins-and-games/prizes-and-winners).
- **Settings**, **Email**: branding, sending and notifications. See [Emails](/emails/).
- **Help & Tools**, **Setup Wizard**: a guided way to set many of these at once. See [Run the Setup Wizard](/getting-started/run-the-setup-wizard).

The effect of each setting is described in its own guide.

## Tips

- Save before you leave the page. Your changes are not kept otherwise.
- Check your site after changing anything that customers see.
- Keep a note of what you change and when.

## Common questions

**Why can I not see a section?**
Some sections are for Raffinity only. They are not covered here.

**Can a moderator open this page?**
No. It is for admins only.

**Is there a setting for how often background jobs run?**
That is managed by Raffinity.
