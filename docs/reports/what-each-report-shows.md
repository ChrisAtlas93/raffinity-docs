# What each report shows

Each page of the reporting dashboard answers a different question. This guide lists what is on each one, so you know where to look. See [Open and use your reports](/reports/open-and-use-reports) for how to move around.

## Home: the daily glance

Your quick look at how things are going.

- Cards for **Revenue**, **Net revenue**, **Orders**, **Tickets sold**, **New customers**, **Avg order value**, **Conversion rate** and **Wallet liability**.
- **Revenue by day**, as a chart or table, against the previous period.
- **Needs attention**, the same things as your **Overview** page.
- A **Live feed** of the latest orders.
- **Top competitions** and **Top sources** for the period.

## Sales

Revenue, orders and refunds.

- Cards for **Revenue**, **Net revenue**, **Orders**, **Tickets sold**, **Avg order value** and **Refunds**.
- **Revenue and orders by day.**
- **Where the money came from**, the payment mix.
- **Revenue by channel**, and **New vs returning customers**. Purchases that started from a push notification show as **Push notifications**.
- **Sales by device**: orders and revenue for **App** (your installed app), **Mobile**, **Tablet** and **Desktop**. Orders with no recorded visit show as **Unknown**.
- Sales by hour and weekday, top competitions by revenue, and tables for card payments, card refunds, chargebacks and all orders placed.
- **Profit and margin** is coming soon. It needs the **Prize cost** filled in on each competition.

![The Sales report](/screenshots/reports-sales.png)

## Marketing and attribution

Where customers come from and what they are worth.

- Tabs: **Sources**, **Channels**, **Funnel**, **Cohorts**, **Audience**, **Ad spend**, **Promos** and **Early Bird**.
- Choose **Last touch** or **First touch**. Last touch gives the credit to the last campaign link a customer used before buying. First touch credits how they first found you.
- Cards for **Ad spend**, **Revenue from ad campaigns**, **Blended ROAS**, **Blended CPA** and **New customers**.
- A table by source, medium and campaign, **Channel mix**, the **Purchase funnel** (visits are estimates), and **Cohort retention**.

For the spend and sources to line up, see [Build tracked links and record ad spend](/marketing-and-tracking/tracked-links-and-ad-spend).

![The Marketing report](/screenshots/reports-marketing.png)

## Competitions

A league table of your competitions, as cards or a table, with what is drawing soon, what is under-filled, **Early Bird** uptake, and instant wins won against configured. Open a competition for its own page, with tabs for **Overview**, **Sales by day**, **Offers and prizes** and **Info**.

![The Competitions report](/screenshots/reports-competitions.png)

## Customers

Growth, value and segments: **Segments**, **Top customers**, the **best channels by customer value**, **Cohort retention**, **Countries and devices** and **Account restrictions**.

![The Customers report](/screenshots/reports-customers.png)

## Wallet and Credit

The money you owe customers: **Money in and out**, what was issued and spent by category, **Largest balances**, the **Payout queue** and **Held and at risk**. **Expected withdrawals** is coming soon.

![The Wallet and Credit report](/screenshots/reports-wallet.png)

## Games and Wins

Instant wins, sets and mini games: **Prizes by type**, **Payout vs revenue by competition**, **Recent wins** and **Collect sets**. **Integrity** is coming soon.

![The Games and Wins report](/screenshots/reports-games.png)

## Promotions

How your offers perform: **Promo codes**, **Automatic offers** and **Early Bird**. **Multibuy offers** is coming soon.

![The Promotions report](/screenshots/reports-promotions.png)

## Reports Library

Your **Saved views**, **Scheduled** reports and **Templates**. Saved views are kept in your browser only. Scheduled reports and export history are coming soon.

## Settings

A place for imports, connections, KPI targets, access and your data and brand. Most of it is coming soon.

## What your customers will see

Nothing. Reports are for you only.

## Tips

- Check **Sales** against your **Overview** if a number surprises you.
- Look at **Marketing** after a campaign ends, not while it is still running.
- Fill in **Prize cost** on competitions to unlock profit figures when they arrive.

## Common questions

**What is the difference between Revenue and Net revenue?**
Net revenue is revenue after refunds.

**Why does a page say Coming soon?**
That part is not built yet. The rest of the page works.

**Where do I see which orders are behind a figure?**
Open **Orders** in the admin. Several report pages link straight to the matching screens.
