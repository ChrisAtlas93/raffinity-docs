# Open and use your reports

The reporting dashboard is where you see how your business is doing: sales, customers, competitions, wallets and marketing. It opens on your website and is for admins only. Moderators do not see it.

## Before you start

- Reports are built from your orders, so a new site shows little until you have sales.
- Figures can lag by a few minutes.

## Open the dashboard

1. In the admin menu, click **Reports**. It opens in a new browser tab.
2. If your browser has not signed in to your site, you may be asked to sign in once.

To go back, click **Back to admin** at the top left.

![The reporting Home page](/screenshots/reports-home.png)

## Find your way around

The menu on the left has: **Home**, **Sales**, **Marketing**, **Competitions**, **Customers**, **Wallet and Credit**, **Games and Wins**, **Promotions**, **Reports Library** and **Settings**. Each page is described in [What each report shows](/reports/what-each-report-shows). You can collapse the menu with **Collapse**.

## Choose a date range

Click the date button at the top, for example **Last 7 days vs previous**. You can choose:

- **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **This month** or **Last month**.
- A **Custom range**, with your own start and end dates.

You can also choose what to compare against: the previous period, the same period last year, or nothing. Most figures show how they have changed against that comparison. A range that starts on the 1st is compared with the same dates last month. While a period is still running, the comparison stops at the same time of day, so a morning is never set against a full day.

The date range and filters stay in the page address as you move between pages, so you can share a link to exactly what you are looking at.

## Search and jump

Click the search box at the top, or press Ctrl and K. It lets you jump to a page, change the date range or apply a filter.

## Read a chart

Hover over a chart to see the figure for that day. Switch between **Chart** and **Table**. Click a day to see its figures.

## Filter

On pages that support it, click **+ Filter** to narrow what you see, for example by source, channel or competition.

## Save and share a view

1. Set the dates and filters you want.
2. Click **Save this view**.

Saved views are kept in your browser only. Open them from **Saved views** at the top, or from the **Reports Library**.

## Export

Click **Export** at the top, or **CSV** on a table, to download the figures.

## The live counter

At the top, a counter shows how many orders came in during the last hour.

## Report emails and saved files

Choose **Schedule email** on any page, or **New report email** in the **Reports Library**. Pick a name, the page to link to, how often and which staff get it. Emails go out at 07:00: **Daily** covers yesterday, **Weekly** goes on Mondays for the last 7 days, and **Monthly** goes on the 1st for last month. Each email has the headline figures and a link to the live page. It can only go to staff who can open the reports.

Any table can be saved with its **CSV** button. On **Sales**, **All orders CSV** saves every order in the date range. Every file saved is listed under **Recent exports** in the Reports Library, with who saved it and when, because some hold customer emails.

## What is not built yet

**Connections** is marked **COMING SOON** and is on the way.

## What your customers will see

Nothing. Reports are for you only.

## Tips

- Start on **Home** each morning, then look at **Sales**.
- Compare against the previous period to see whether a change is good or bad.
- Use **Custom range** to line up a report with a campaign.

## Common questions

**Why are my figures lower than the admin shows?**
Reports can lag by a few minutes.

**Why can I not open Reports?**
It is for admins only.

**Can I email myself a report?**
Not yet. Use **Export**.
