# Answer messages and handle complaints

Customers can write to you in two ways: a contact form for general questions, and a complaints form for problems. Both arrive in **Support & Safety**. This guide covers how to work through them.

## Before you start

- Moderators can use both screens by default. The owner can switch that off in **Settings**, then **Business & Site Settings**, under **Screens moderators can open**.
- Set who is told when a message or complaint arrives, under **Settings**, then **Email**, then **Notifications**. See [Emails](/emails/). In particular, set the recipients for **Complaint Received**, or nobody is told.

## Contact messages

1. Open **Support & Safety**, then **Contact Messages**. A number on the menu shows how many are unread.
2. Each row shows whether it is **Read**, when it was **Received**, the name, email, subject and message.
3. Click **View** to read it. Use **Mark read** or **Mark unread** to keep track, and **Delete** to remove one.

![The Contact Messages screen](/screenshots/contact-messages.png)

### Answer a message

There is no reply button in the admin. When a message arrives, your team gets a **Contact Form Message** email. Reply to that email, and your reply goes straight to the customer.

### What customers use

Customers write to you using the contact form on your Contact page. It asks for their **Your name**, **Email address**, an optional **Subject** and their **Message**, and thanks them when it is sent.

## Complaints

1. Open **Support & Safety**, then **Complaints**. A number on the menu shows how many are new.
2. Each row shows the **reference**, when it was **Received**, the name, email and topic, and its status. Filter by status.

![The Complaints screen](/screenshots/complaints.png)

3. Click **Open** to read it. The top section, **What the customer told us**, is read only. It has the reference, name, email, phone, the order or competition they mention, the topic and their message.
4. In **What we did**:
   - Set the **Status**: **New**, **Investigating**, **Resolved** or **Escalated**.
   - Write the **Outcome and what the customer was told**. It is kept as the record of how the complaint was handled.
5. Save. Choosing **Resolved** or **Escalated** records the time, and the staff member who saved is recorded.

Reply to the customer by email yourself. Complaints are never deleted.

### What customers see

Customers complain at `/complaints` on your site. The page is headed **Make a complaint**. It asks for their name, email, an optional phone number, an optional order or competition, a topic and what happened. The topics are: a draw or its result, a prize, a payment, refund or Site Credit, my account, limits, breaks or self-exclusion, and something else.

They get a reference such as C-7K3QX9 on screen, and an acknowledgement email that promises someone will review what they told you and reply, and that you will tell them where else they can take it. Staff get a **Complaint Received** email.

The platform does not promise a response time. Decide yours, and say so in your own wording under **Website**, then **Site Wording**, in the item "Accountability and complaints".

## What your customers will see

Their message is acknowledged, and they hear from you by email.

## Tips

- Answer messages quickly. A short holding reply is better than silence.
- Record the outcome on every complaint, even a simple one.
- Check **Complaints** every day.

## Common questions

**Why did nobody tell me about a complaint?**
Check the recipients of the **Complaint Received** email under **Settings**, **Email**, **Notifications**.

**Can I delete a complaint?**
No. A complaint is kept with its status and outcome.

**Where do I find a customer's orders?**
Search for their email on the **Customers** screen. See [Find and manage customers](/customers/find-and-manage-customers).
