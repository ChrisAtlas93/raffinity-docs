# Deal with stop marketing requests

Anyone can ask you to stop marketing to them, whether or not they have an account. Your site handles part of it for you. The rest is up to you. This guide explains which is which.

## Before you start

- **Stop Marketing Requests** is for admins only, and view only. You cannot create, edit or delete a request.
- Make sure someone is listed to receive the **Stop Marketing Request** email, under **Settings**, **Email**, **Notifications**.

## What customers see

At `/stop-marketing` on your site, a page headed **Stop marketing** asks for an **Email address**, a **Phone number** and an optional name. They click **Stop marketing to me** and see "Request received".

## What happens automatically

- Any account matching the email or phone number is marked "do not market" straight away.
- Your team is sent a **Stop Marketing Request** email.
- The request is recorded in your admin.

## See the requests

Open **Support & Safety**, then **Stop Marketing Requests**. Each row shows when it was **Received**, the email, phone and name, whether an **Account found**, and the IP address.

![The Stop Marketing Requests screen](/screenshots/stop-marketing-requests.png)

## What you must do yourself

Your site cannot reach outside tools. If you use an email marketing tool such as Klaviyo, Mailchimp, Brevo or MailerLite, or any other list, **remove the person from it by hand**. Do this for every request.

## Who is never marketed to

Your site never sends marketing to anyone who has opted out, is on a break, self-excluded or closed, is on a purchase hold, or has an amber or red welfare flag.

## Can a customer start marketing again?

A customer who opted out with the form can say yes to marketing again by ticking the marketing box in their account or at checkout. If they opted out because they self-excluded or closed their account, or because staff opted them out, they cannot undo it that way.

## What your customers will see

A confirmation on screen, and no more marketing.

## Tips

- Act on every request on the day it arrives.
- Keep your own list of who you have removed from outside tools.

## Common questions

**Does this stop emails about their orders?**
No. It only stops marketing. Emails about their orders and account still go out.

**Why does a request say no account found?**
The person has no account on your site. You still need to remove them from your own lists.
