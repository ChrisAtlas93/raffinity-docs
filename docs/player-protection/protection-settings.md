# Set when players are flagged

Welfare monitoring flags a player when their activity reaches levels you set. This guide shows how to turn it on and choose those levels. It is for admins only.

## Before you start

- Decide who should be told when a player turns red, and add them to the **Player Welfare Alert** email under **Settings**, then **Email**, then **Notifications**. See [Emails](/emails/).
- Read [How player protection works](/player-protection/how-it-works).

## Open the settings

Open **Settings**, then **Business Rules**, then the **Player Protection** tab. Click **Save Settings** when you finish.

![The Player Protection settings](/screenshots/player-protection-settings.png)

## Monitoring

- **Welfare monitoring is on.** Switch it on to watch for signs of harm. Amber gently nudges the player and keeps marketing off. Red does the same and also alerts staff.
- **Place a purchase hold automatically when a player reaches red.** Off by default, so a person looks first. A hold stops buying and top-ups only. Staff can release it from **Support & Safety**, **Player Welfare**.
- **This many amber signs at once count as red.** From 2 to 9.

## When to flag

A player reaches amber or red when any one of these is met. Set a level to 0 to switch that sign off. These are the starting levels, shown as amber then red:

| Sign | Amber | Red |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spend in the last 7 days | £500 | £1,500 |
| Spend in the last 30 days | £1,500 | £5,000 |
| This week against their usual week (a multiple) | 3x | 6x |
| ...but only once this week is above | £100 | £250 |
| Orders in the last 24 hours | 8 | 20 |
| Orders between midnight and 6am (14 days) | 5 | 12 |
| Times they hit their own spend limit (30 days) | 3 | 8 |
| Different ways of paying (30 days) | 3 | 5 |
| Breaks or self-exclusions started (30 days) | 2 | 4 |
| Spend limits raised or removed (90 days) | 1 | 2 |

A player's "usual week" is the average of the 3 weeks before this one.

## What your customers will see

An amber or red player sees a check-in message in their account, and their marketing stops. If you have switched on automatic holds, a red player also cannot buy or top up until staff release the hold.

## Tips

- Start with the standard levels. Look at the flags for a few weeks before you change them.
- A level that is too low flags too many players. A level that is too high misses people.
- Check that the right people receive the **Player Welfare Alert** email.

## Common questions

**Can I switch off one sign only?**
Yes. Set its levels to 0.

**Does amber contact the customer?**
They see a gentle check-in message. Nobody is emailed.

**Where do I see who has been flagged?**
In **Support & Safety**, **Player Welfare**.
