# How player protection works

Player protection is a set of tools that help customers stay in control of how much they spend, and help you spot customers who may need a check-in. This guide explains what customers can do themselves, and what happens on your side.

## Before you start

- Read your **Responsible Play** wording under **Website**, then **Site Wording**, so what you tell customers matches what the tools do.
- Player protection is set up in **Settings**, **Business Rules**, **Player Protection**. See [Set when players are flagged](/player-protection/protection-settings).

## What customers can set themselves

Customers find these in their account, under **Spending Controls**.

- **Spend limits.** A **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly** limit in pounds, from £0 up. The day is the calendar day, the week starts on Monday and the month is the calendar month, in UK time. What counts is what the customer paid, less refunds. Orders placed by staff are not counted. Top-ups are checked against the limits too.
- **Pause Your Account.** A break of 1 day, 1 week, 1 month or until a date they choose. It ends at 23:59 UK time on the chosen date. It can be extended but never shortened. It stops buying, and marketing emails stop while it lasts. Moving Cash Wallet money into Site Credit is still allowed.
- **Self-Exclusion.** At least 6 months: 6 months, 1 year, 5 years, or a date. It can be extended but never shortened. It stops buying, top-ups and converting cash to Site Credit, and it stops marketing for good. Withdrawals still work. When it ends, the customer must confirm before playing again, and they are emailed once.
- **Close Your Account.** The customer types CLOSE to confirm. Only staff can reopen it. Sign-in and withdrawals still work.

A customer who is self-excluded or closed cannot change their phone number, and a new registration with a phone number that belongs to one is refused.

The platform also looks for the same person under different details. If a new account has the same date of birth as a self-excluded or closed account, together with the same phone number or the same postcode, it is let in but put under the same exclusion straight away. The customer is not told why. You are emailed (to the recipients of **Excluded Player Match** under **Email**, **Notifications**, or to every admin if none are set), and the account's spending controls history shows what matched. If it turns out to be a different person, an admin can remove the exclusion in the customer's spending controls, which takes effect after the 24 hour cooling-off. See [Change a customer's spending controls](/player-protection/spend-controls-and-welfare).

### Raising a limit

Customers can only make their limits **lower**. They cannot raise or remove a limit themselves. They are told to contact you. When you raise a limit for them, it takes effect after a 24 hour cooling-off period. Lowering one takes effect straight away. See [Change a customer's spending controls](/player-protection/spend-controls-and-welfare).

## What your site does for you

- **Welfare monitoring.** Your site watches for signs of harm, such as high spending or many orders late at night. When a customer reaches a level you have set, they are flagged **amber** or **red**.
- **Amber.** The customer sees a gentle check-in message and their marketing stops.
- **Red.** The same, and your team is emailed. A purchase hold is placed automatically only if you have switched that on.

Staff review the flags in **Support & Safety**, **Player Welfare**. See [Change a customer's spending controls](/player-protection/spend-controls-and-welfare).

## Who never gets marketing

A customer does not get marketing if they have opted out, are on a break, self-excluded or closed, are on a purchase hold, or have an amber or red flag.

## What your customers will see

A **Spending Controls** page in their account, a status badge showing whether they are active or on a break, and check-in messages if they are flagged. Your **Responsible Play** page describes these tools.

## Tips

- Check **Player Welfare** every day.

## Common questions

**Can I change a customer's limit for them?**
Yes. See the next guide. Lowering it applies at once. Raising it takes 24 hours.

**Can a self-excluded customer withdraw their money?**
Yes. Withdrawals still work for self-excluded and closed accounts.

**Can a customer end their self-exclusion early?**
No. It can only be extended.
