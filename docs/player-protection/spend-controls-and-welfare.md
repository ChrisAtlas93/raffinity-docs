# Change a customer's spending controls and review welfare flags

Two admin screens help you look after players. **Spend Controls** lets you change a customer's limits. **Player Welfare** is where flagged customers appear for review. Both are for admins only.

## Before you start

- Read [How player protection works](/player-protection/how-it-works).
- A row only exists on **Spend Controls** once the customer has opened their Spending Controls page, tried to buy, or had a hold placed. You cannot set limits for a customer who has no row yet.

## Change a customer's spending controls

1. Open **Customers**, then **Spend Controls**.
2. Click **Edit** on the customer's row. Use **Search** to find them.
3. Change what you need:
   - **Status.** Active, Break, Self-excluded or Closed. The status shown on the list is worked out from the dates below.
   - **Daily limit (£)**, **Weekly limit (£)** and **Monthly limit (£)**. Leave empty for no limit.
   - **Break until**, **Self-excluded until** and **Account closed at**. Times are Europe/London.
   - **Admin note (stored in change log).** Say why.
4. Click **Save changes**.

![Editing a customer's spend controls](/screenshots/spend-controls-edit.png)

### Tightening and loosening

- A change that **tightens** a control, such as lowering a limit or starting a break, applies straight away. You see "Changes applied".
- A change that **loosens** one waits 24 hours. You see "Change scheduled (cooling-off)" and the time it will take effect. Loosening means raising or removing a limit, shortening or removing a break or exclusion, reopening a closed account, or setting the status back to Active.
- If the customer changes the same control in the meantime, their change wins.

The customer is emailed about every change, whoever makes it. The **Changes** list on the page records each change, who made it and its status.

## Review welfare flags

1. Open **Support & Safety**, then **Player Welfare**. The page opens on **Red** flags. Use the filters for tier (**Red**, **Amber** or **Cleared**) and status (**Needs a look** or **Reviewed**).
2. Click **Open** on a flag. It shows who the player is, what was noticed (each indicator and its level), and your review.
3. Decide what to do, and use:
   - **Mark reviewed.** Say **What did you decide?**. This is required.
   - **Place purchase hold.** Stops buying and top-ups only. The customer is told to contact you. Give a reason, which is kept in the audit log.
   - **Release hold.** Lifts the hold. Add a note.

![The Player Welfare screen](/screenshots/player-welfare.png)

The **Audit log** tab on a flag records everything that happened: when it was flagged, escalated, reviewed, held or released, and who did it.

If a flagged player gets worse after you reviewed it, the flag reopens.

### How flags are raised

After each paid order, at most once every 5 minutes for a player, and in a full check every night, your site compares each player with the levels you set. Any one red sign makes a player red. Three or more amber signs at once also count as red, and you can change that number.

## Raise a welfare concern by hand

If you are worried about a customer but nothing has flagged them:

1. On the **Customers** list, open the row's **More actions** menu for the customer.
2. Click **Raise welfare concern**.
3. Type **What is the concern?** and click **Raise concern**.

The customer appears in **Player Welfare** as red until a person reviews it. Nothing changes for them unless you then place a purchase hold. A concern raised by hand does not send an alert email. Moderators can raise a concern, but cannot open **Player Welfare**.

## What your customers will see

- **Amber or red.** A check-in message in their account, and their marketing stops.
- **A purchase hold.** They cannot buy or top up, and are told to contact you.
- **A change to their controls.** An email, and the new limits in their Spending Controls page.

## Tips

- Take a flag as a reason to talk, not to punish.
- Write a clear note whenever you mark a flag reviewed or place a hold.
- Check the **Changes** list before you answer a customer who says their limit changed.

## Common questions

**Why does my change say it is scheduled?**
It loosens a control, so it waits 24 hours.

**Can I cancel a scheduled change?**
There is no button to cancel one.

**Does a purchase hold stop withdrawals?**
No. It stops buying and top-ups only.
