# What happens after a draw

When a draw is applied, the competition becomes **Completed**, the winners are saved, and the winners are emailed. This guide covers what you and your customers see, what happens when a competition does not sell out, and how to reopen one.

## Before you start

- The draw must have been applied. See [Close a competition and draw the winner](/draws-and-winners/run-a-draw).

## What the winner and you receive

- **Winners.** Each winner is emailed their own place. This happens whichever way the draw was made.
- **You.** The staff emails you have switched on for completed draws are sent. See [Emails](/emails/).
- **Prizes.** If the main prize is a physical item, you arrange it with the winner yourself. The cash alternative is shown on the page. You also arrange that with the winner.

## See the draw record

Open the completed competition in the admin. The **Details** tab now has two extra sections:

- **Winner.** Who won, and the ticket number, and when the winners were selected.
- **Draw record.** How the draw was run: the **Method**, **Tickets in the draw**, **Run by**, a **Fingerprint of the entry list (SHA-256)**, the **Entry list PDF**, a **Note and recording**, and **Instant wins at the draw**, which shows how many winning tickets there were and whether staff changed any after the competition opened.

The entry list PDF is made a few minutes after the draw.

## What your customers will see

- The competition page shows a **Winner announced** panel with the winner's name, in the format you chose, and the winning ticket number.
- For draws run with the provably fair method, a **How this winner was chosen** card explains the draw, with **Verify this draw**, where anyone can re-check it in their browser, and **Download the proof**.
- Older draws show a simpler record: how the winner was chosen, tickets in the draw, the winning ticket and when it was run, with the entry list.
- Your **Winners** and **Entry lists** pages list the finished competitions.

How names appear follows your winner name settings. See [Send prizes and show winners](/instant-wins-and-games/prizes-and-winners).

## If a competition does not sell out

- The draw goes ahead at the end time. There is no minimum number of tickets to sell.
- If **nothing at all** has sold by the end time, there is nothing to draw. The competition goes back to **Draft**, and you are told, so you can edit it and run it again.

Check that your **Site Wording** says what you actually do. It has items for "When draws are held" and "If a competition does not sell out". See [Your website](/your-website/).

## Refund an order after a draw

Once a competition has been drawn, an order that entered it cannot be cancelled in the usual way. The options are **Refund, keep entries** or **Goodwill credit**. See [Orders and refunds](/orders-and-refunds/).

## Reopen a competition

You can reopen a closed or completed competition, for example if it was closed by mistake.

1. Open the competition and click **More actions**, then **Reopen**.
2. Enter a **New sales end date** and a **New draw date**, and choose whether to **Enable autodraw on reopen**.
3. Confirm.

Reopening deletes the winners and the draw record, and sets the tickets back to active. Be careful. Do not do this to a competition whose winner has already been told.

## Tips

- After a draw, look at the **Draw record** to confirm everything is as expected.
- Check your **Prize Claims** if the competition had instant wins. See [Send prizes and show winners](/instant-wins-and-games/prizes-and-winners).

## Common questions

**Can I change the winner after the draw?**
No. A finished draw cannot be re-rolled. Reopening removes the result and starts again.

**Where do customers check a draw?**
On the competition page, with **Verify this draw**, or at your site's verify page.

**What if a customer asks for a refund after a draw?**
See [Orders and refunds](/orders-and-refunds/). Entries are kept after a draw.
