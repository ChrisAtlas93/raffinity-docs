# Close a competition and draw the winner

When ticket sales end, the competition closes and waits for its draw. You can let the platform draw the winner for you with **autodraw**, or run the draw yourself. Both use the provably fair draw, which customers can check afterwards.

## Before you start

- A moderator can only close and draw competitions if the owner has switched on "Close a competition early and draw its winner". Admins can always do it.
- The competition must be **Closed**. It closes by itself at the end time or when it sells out.
- A competition cannot be drawn while its tickets are still being given to customers. If the draw says it cannot run yet, wait a minute and try again.

## Check what is waiting

On your **Overview** page, the **Draws due** card in **Needs you** shows competitions that are closed and not yet drawn, and how long they have been waiting. In **Competitions**, the **Awaiting draw** tab lists them too.

## Let the platform draw it (autodraw)

1. When you create or edit the competition, switch on **Enable autodraw** in the **Schedule & Status** panel.
2. When the competition closes, by selling out or reaching its end time, the winner is drawn automatically a short while later using the provably fair draw.
3. The winner is emailed, and you are emailed a summary.

If an automatic draw fails more than once, you are emailed an alert and can finish it yourself from the competition page.

## Close a competition early

1. Open the competition, click **More actions**, then **Close Now**. You can also use **Close now** in the row menu on the **Competitions** list.
2. Confirm. Sales stop straight away. If **Enable autodraw** is on, the draw follows.

## Run the provably fair draw yourself

You run it from your website, as a signed-in staff member.

1. Open the closed competition on your live site. In the admin, use **View Competition** on its edit page, or **View on website** in the list's row menu.
2. Open the staff toolbar at the bottom left and click **Run the draw**. A **Run the draw (staff only)** button on the page itself does the same.

![The staff toolbar on a closed competition](/screenshots/draw-staff-toolbar.png)

3. On the first step, **Set up**, answer **How should we draw?**
   - **Sold tickets only.** The draw is from the tickets that were bought.
   - **All tickets.** The draw is from every ticket number, sold or not. If you choose this, you also choose **If an unsold number comes up**: **Move on to the next number** or **No winner**.
4. Optionally open **Add a note or a recording link** to keep a note with the draw.
5. Click **Lock and draw**. After this, the entries cannot change and the draw cannot be re-rolled.

![The draw set-up step](/screenshots/draw-popup-setup.png)

6. The draw waits for a public random number. The screen counts down and shows the checks it makes.
7. When it is ready, click **Reveal the winner**. For several winners, the places are revealed from the last place to the first.
8. Click **Apply the result**, then confirm with **Yes, apply the result**. This finishes the competition. It cannot be undone.

The winner is not shown on the page or sent to the browser until you reveal it.

### Cancel a draw

You can use **Cancel the draw** only before the random number has been published. A reason is required, and the cancelled attempt stays on the public record. Once the result is ready, it cannot be re-rolled.

### Draw the next competition

When a draw finishes, **Draw this next** offers the next closed competition that is waiting, and keeps your choice of mode.

## More than one winner

Set **Number of winners** on the competition's **Details** tab. A higher number draws that many different tickets. Two switches shape it:

- **A customer can win more than once.** On means every winning ticket wins, even if one customer holds several. Off means each customer can take only one place.
- **Winners win different prizes** with **Prize for each place**, so each place can have its own prize.

These fields are locked once a winner has been drawn.

## The older Draw Winner button

**More actions** on a closed competition also has **Draw Winner**, an older method where a number is picked or typed in. Use the provably fair draw instead, so customers can check the result.

## What your customers will see

Once the competition is closed, the page shows that sales have closed and the draw is awaited. After the draw it shows the winner. See [What happens after a draw](/draws-and-winners/after-the-draw).

## Tips

- Turn on autodraw for competitions you do not want to draw by hand.
- Add a note or a recording link if you stream your draws.
- Check **Draws due** each day, so no competition waits too long.

## Common questions

**Why can I not see Run the draw?**
It only shows on a closed competition that has not been drawn, and only to staff who are allowed to draw.

**Does the draw wait if tickets are still being issued?**
Yes. It will not run until every order has its tickets.

**Can I draw a competition that did not sell out?**
Yes. The draw goes ahead at the end time. See [What happens after a draw](/draws-and-winners/after-the-draw#if-a-competition-does-not-sell-out).
