# Wallets and withdrawals

Customers can hold money on your site in a Cash Wallet and as Site Credit. This section explains both, and how to set the rules, manage balances and handle withdrawals.

## The wallet

- [Understand the wallet and set your wallet rules](/wallets-and-withdrawals/wallet-rules). Cash Wallet, Site Credit, rewards, points and conversion.
- [Manage wallets and top-ups](/wallets-and-withdrawals/manage-wallets-and-topups). Adjust balances, let customers top up and reverse a top-up.
- [Approve and pay withdrawals](/wallets-and-withdrawals/withdrawals). Checks, ID, bank details, paying and rejecting.
