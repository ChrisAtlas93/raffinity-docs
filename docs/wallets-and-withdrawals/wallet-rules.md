# Understand the wallet and set your wallet rules

Every customer can hold money on your site in two places. This guide explains them, and shows you how to set the rules for the wallet, including rewards and points. You can rename both to suit your brand.

## Before you start

- Check that **Customer wallet** and the features you want are switched on. See [Switch features on and off](/getting-started/switch-features-on-and-off).
- You need to be an admin.

## Cash Wallet and Site Credit

- **Cash Wallet.** Money a customer can **withdraw** to their bank account. They cannot spend it on tickets directly. They can convert it to Site Credit.
- **Site Credit.** Money a customer can **spend on tickets** but cannot withdraw.

Money gets into a wallet in these ways:

- Instant win prizes paid as **Cash Wallet** or **Site Credit**.
- Leaderboard prizes, which are paid to the Cash Wallet.
- A **top-up** by card, which adds Site Credit. See [Manage wallets and top-ups](/wallets-and-withdrawals/manage-wallets-and-topups).
- Refunds that cannot go back to a card, and goodwill credits, which go to the Cash Wallet.
- Rewards such as a welcome bonus, cashback, referral credit and loyalty milestones.
- A staff adjustment.

Customers can convert Cash Wallet money into Site Credit, but never the other way. Wallet money does not expire. Only points can.

## Open the wallet rules

Open **Settings**, then **Business Rules**, then the **Wallet Rules** tab. Click **Save Settings** when you finish.

![The Wallet Rules page](/screenshots/wallet-rules.png)

## Name your wallets and set how they work

In **Cash Wallet & Site Credit**:

- **Cash Wallet name** and **Site Credit name.** Rename them if you want. Your site and emails use your names.
- **Allow converting Cash Wallet to Site Credit.** Customers can move cash into Site Credit. The conversion cannot be undone.
- **Conversion bonus (%).** For example, 10 means converting £50 gives £55 of Site Credit. 0 means no bonus.
- **Minimum conversion (£).**
- **Allow paying with Site Credit at checkout.** Any remainder is paid by card. You can exclude individual competitions on their **Tickets & pricing** tab.
- **Apply Site Credit automatically at checkout.** Customers can still untick it.

## Rewards

Each reward has an **Enabled** switch.

- **Welcome Bonus.** Set the **Amount (£)** new customers receive.
- **Cashback.** Set the **Percentage (%)** of what customers spend that comes back as Site Credit.
- **Referral Programme.** Choose the **Referral trigger**: **First order only**, **One time per referrer** or **Every purchase forever**. Set the **Referrer Credit (£)**, **Referee Credit (£)** and the **Minimum order to count (£)**. An order below the minimum, paid in real money, does not reward the referrer. 0 means any amount counts.
- **Loyalty Milestones.** Click **Add Milestone** and set a **Cumulative Spend (£)** and a **Reward (£)**. Each milestone triggers once per customer, by their lifetime spend.
- **Points System.** Set **Points per £1 Spent**, **Points per £1 Reward** (for example 100 means 100 points converts to £1) and **Points Expiry (days)**. 0 means never expire.

When an order is cancelled and refunded, cashback, points and any referral reward earned on it are taken back. Loyalty milestone rewards are not.

## Top-ups

See [Manage wallets and top-ups](/wallets-and-withdrawals/manage-wallets-and-topups).

## Withdrawals

In **Withdrawals**, switch on **Allow Withdrawals** and set the **Minimum Amount (£)**. See [Approve and pay withdrawals](/wallets-and-withdrawals/withdrawals).

## What your customers will see

A wallet page in their account with their Cash Wallet and Site Credit, their history, and buttons to convert, top up and withdraw, depending on what you have switched on. At checkout, they can pay with Site Credit and the rest by card.

## Tips

- Use the names your customers already know.

## Common questions

**Can customers spend Cash Wallet money on tickets?**
Not directly. They convert it to Site Credit first.

**Does the money expire?**
No. Only points can have an expiry.

**Where did my wallet switches go?**
They are on this page, and the main on and off switches are in the Setup Wizard's features step.
