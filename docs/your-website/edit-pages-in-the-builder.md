# Edit your pages with the site builder

The site builder lets you change what your pages show, without touching any code. You can rearrange sections, switch them on or off, add new ones, and schedule changes. This guide shows how. It is for admins only.

## Before you start

- You need to be an admin. Anyone else who opens the builder sees that it is for admin accounts.
- The builder opens on your website. If it asks you to sign in, use your admin details.

## See your pages

Open **Website**, then **Pages**. The list has these groups:

- **Main pages.** Your **Home** page, and **Every competition page**, one layout shared by all competitions.
- **About, Contact and FAQ.** **About Us**, **Contact Us** and **FAQ**. Each has **Edit in builder**, **View** and **Details**. Contact also has **Messages**, and FAQ has **Edit questions**.
- **Pages you have made.** Your own pages, which live at `/p/` followed by the page name.
- **Legal and policy pages.** Plain text pages with their own editor. See [Edit your wording, FAQs and legal pages](/your-website/wording-faqs-and-legal).

Each page shows a status: **Live**, **Unpublished changes**, **Not live yet** or **Hidden**.

![The Pages screen](/screenshots/pages-list.png)

## Open the builder

Click **Edit in builder** beside a page.

![The site builder on the home page](/screenshots/builder-home.png)

Across the top you will find:

- A page picker, and **Website**, which takes you back to your site.
- **New page.**
- Buttons for **Computer**, **Tablet** and **Phone** previews.
- A **Preview as** menu: **Editing (every section)**, **As a visitor who is signed out** or **As a signed-in customer**.
- **Undo** and **Redo**, and a note showing **Draft saved**.
- **History**, **Live site**, **Schedule** and **Publish**.

Your changes are saved as a draft. They do not appear on your live site until you click **Publish**.

## Rearrange and hide sections

1. On the left, the **Sections** tab lists the sections on the page, in order.
2. Drag a section, or use its arrows, to move it.
3. Click the eye to hide a section on the live site, without removing it.
4. Click **Publish** when you are happy.

Your home page starts with sections such as **Home banner**, **Trust bar**, **Competitions grid**, **More ways to win**, **How it works**, **Recent winners**, **Live instant wins**, **Join the community** and a closing call to action.

The competition page has its own sections, including **Top of the competition page** (always needed), **How the winner was chosen**, **Try me now**, **Leaderboard**, **Instant wins**, **Collect the sets**, **Entry list**, **Details and rules** and **Related competitions**.

## Change a section

Click a section in the list, or in the preview. Its settings open on the right.

- **Wording and pictures.** The section's text and images.
- **Look.** Which template draws the section, if you want it to look different from the rest of the site. Changing the look updates your live site straight away.
- **When it shows.**
  - **Show this section on the live site.**
  - **Who sees it.**
  - **Which screens.** **Computers**, **Tablets** and **Phones**.
  - **Starts showing** and **Stops showing**, to run a section for a set time. Times use your computer's time zone.
- **Remove from page.**

![A section's settings](/screenshots/builder-section.png)

## Add a section

1. Click the **Add** tab.
2. Choose a block, such as an image banner, promo banner, countdown, text and picture, video, questions and answers, partner logos, chosen competitions, category tabs, page heading, text, contact form and details, site FAQs or custom HTML.
3. Fill in its settings.

Custom HTML is cleaned for safety. Scripts, styles and frames are removed.

![Adding a block](/screenshots/builder-add.png)

## Publish, schedule and go back

- **Publish.** Puts your draft on the live site.
- **Discard draft.** Throws away changes you have not published.
- **Schedule.** Choose when your changes go live, with **Goes live** and **Goes back**, and an option to go back to what was live before.
- **History.** Lists earlier versions. Choose one and click **Go back to this version**.

If two admins edit the same page at once, the builder warns you and lets you choose **Keep mine** or **Load their version**.

## Make a new page

1. On the **Pages** screen, or in the builder, click **Add page** or **New page**.
2. Type a **Page name** and a **Short description (for search engines)**.
3. Click **Create and open the builder**.
4. Build the page, then click **Publish**. A new page is not public until you publish it.

The page lives at `/p/` followed by its name. To delete a page you made, use **Delete** on the **Pages** screen.

## Change the look of the whole site

The **Site** tab in the builder changes your template, colours, fonts and width for the whole site. It has no draft. **Apply to the live site** puts the change live at once, and **Undo my changes** reverts it. See [Choose your template, colours and logo](/your-website/template-colours-and-logo).

## What your customers will see

Your published pages, with the sections you have chosen, in the order you set. Hidden or scheduled sections do not show.

## Tips

- Use the **Phone** preview to check how a page looks on a phone.
- Use **Preview as** a visitor who is signed out, to see what a new customer sees.
- Schedule a promotion section to start and stop by itself.

## Common questions

**Why can I not see my change on the live site?**
Click **Publish**. Changes are drafts until then.

**Can I get back an old version?**
Yes. Use **History**.

**Can a moderator use the builder?**
No. It is for admins only.
