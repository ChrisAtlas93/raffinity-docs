# Show a holding page and set your company details

A holding page replaces your whole site with a simple message while you are down for maintenance, or before you launch. This guide also covers your announcement bar and the company details shown in your footer. It is for admins only.

## Before you start

- Open **Settings**, then **Business & Site Settings**. The page is called **Site Settings Page**. These settings are all on it, one section after another. Click **Save Settings** when you finish.

## Show a holding page

1. Find the section **Holding Page**.
2. Switch on **Show the holding page**. This immediately blocks everyone except staff from your site.
3. Choose the **Type**:
   - **Maintenance: we are briefly down and will be back soon.**
   - **Coming soon: the site has not launched yet.**
4. Optionally set the **Heading** and **Message**. Leave them empty to use the standard wording for the type.
5. For Coming soon, set the **Launch date and time (UK time)** to show a countdown, and decide whether to **Collect "Notify me" email addresses**.
6. Click **Save Settings**.

![The Holding Page section](/screenshots/holding-page.png)

While the holding page is on, nobody except staff can browse, sign in or register. Staff can still sign in using the **Team login** link on it.

A coming soon page does **not** switch itself off when the launch date arrives. You must switch off **Show the holding page** yourself. See [Before you go live](/getting-started/before-you-go-live).

### Collect launch sign-ups

If you switched on **Collect "Notify me" email addresses**, people can leave their email. Find them in **Marketing**, then **Launch Notify Sign-ups**, which only shows while the holding page is on or there are sign-ups. Click **Download CSV**.

Your site does not email these people for you. Use your own email tool to tell them you have launched. See [Connect your email marketing tool](/marketing-and-tracking/email-and-push). Visitors are told "Thanks. We'll be in touch when we launch."

## Add an announcement bar

1. Find the section **Announcement bar**.
2. Type up to four lines of up to 120 characters each in **Announcements**.
3. Click **Save Settings**.

The settings say the bar shows on the Neon template. Check how it looks on yours.

![The Announcement bar section](/screenshots/announcement-bar.png)

## Set your company details

1. Find the section **Company & Footer**.
2. Type your **Company Name** and **Company / Registration Number**.
3. Click **Save Settings**.

They appear in the copyright bar in your footer.

![The Company and Footer section](/screenshots/company-footer.png)

## What your customers will see

- With a holding page on, a page with your heading and message, and a "Notify me" box if you collect sign-ups.
- An announcement bar with your lines.
- Your company name and number in your footer.

## Tips

- Use Maintenance for short jobs and Coming soon before launch.
- Check the holding page on a phone.
- Tell your team before you switch the holding page on.

## Common questions

**Why can I still see my site when the holding page is on?**
Staff can still browse. Open your site in a private window to see what visitors see.

**How do I switch the holding page off?**
Switch off **Show the holding page** and save.

**Does a launch date switch the site on?**
No. You must switch it on by hand.
