# Let customers install your site as an app

Customers can add your site to their phone's home screen and use it like an app. You do not have to build or publish anything. This guide explains what they get, and how you can reward them.

## Before you start

- This works from your website. Customers install it from their phone's browser.
- Push notifications are optional. See [Connect your email marketing tool and push notifications](/marketing-and-tracking/email-and-push).

## What customers get

- An install prompt on your site, so they can add it to their home screen.
- A bar at the bottom of the screen with **Home**, **Comps**, **HQ**, **Basket** and **Account**.
- Extra pages for their activity, including their tickets, the competitions they follow and their notifications.
- Push notifications, if you have set them up and they turn them on.

Your app icon comes from **Website**, then **Branding & Appearance**, under **Logo & Favicon**, in **App icon (home screen)**. See [Choose your template, colours and logo](/your-website/template-colours-and-logo).

## Give app users early access

On a competition, in the **Schedule & Status** panel, **Who can enter** has two app choices:

- **App early access.** People using the installed app can buy first. Set **Opens to everyone at**, and everyone else can buy from then.
- **App only.** Only people using the installed app can buy. The competition page tells other customers they need to download the app to enter, with buttons showing how to get it.

See [Choose who can enter](/competitions/choose-who-can-enter).

## What your customers will see

An app-like experience from your site, with your branding. For app-only competitions, a message and buttons that explain how to get the app.

## Tips

- Tell customers how to install your site, for example in an email or a popup.
- Use early access to reward customers who install.
- Check your app icon on a real phone.

## Common questions

**Do I need to publish an app in an app store?**
No. Customers add your site to their home screen.

**Can I see how many people use the app?**
Not in the reports yet.
