# Edit your wording, FAQs and legal pages

Your site explains how it works in several places: How It Works, Responsible Play, your FAQs and your legal pages. This guide shows where to edit that wording, so it always describes how you really run things.

## Before you start

- Moderators can edit site wording and use the media library by default. The owner can change that. Legal pages, and the site builder pages, are for admins.
- Check what you say against what you do. Customers rely on it.

## Edit your site wording

1. Open **Website**, then **Site Wording**.
2. Each piece of wording shows as **Automatic**, which follows your Setup Wizard answers, or **Yours**, once you have edited it.
3. Click **Edit** beside a piece, change it, and click **Save**. **Cancel** closes it. **Reset to automatic** goes back to the standard wording.

![The Site Wording screen](/screenshots/site-wording.png)

The pieces are:

- Site description
- How winners are chosen
- When draws are held
- How winners are announced
- Prize delivery
- Cash alternatives
- If a competition does not sell out
- Refunds
- Skill question
- Free postal entry (short)
- Accountability and complaints
- How to contact you

These appear on How It Works, Responsible Play, your FAQs and Contact page, and in the descriptions that search engines and social media show. Once you edit a piece, it stops following the Setup Wizard.

## Edit your FAQs

1. Open **Website**, then **Pages**.
2. In the **FAQ** row, click **Edit questions**.
3. Add, edit and order your questions.

![The FAQ questions](/screenshots/faq-questions.png)

The page layout of your FAQ page is edited in the builder. See [Edit your pages with the site builder](/your-website/edit-pages-in-the-builder).

## Edit your About and Contact pages

Your **About Us** and **Contact Us** pages are built in the site builder. Use **Edit in builder** beside them on the **Pages** screen. **Details** changes the page's name and search description. The contact form is a block you can place on the page, and messages arrive in **Support & Safety**, **Contact Messages**.

## Edit your legal pages

1. Open **Website**, then **Pages**.
2. Under **Legal and policy pages**, click **Edit** beside the page.
3. Change the page in its sections: **Page Details**, with an **Enabled** switch, **Content** and **SEO**.

![The legal pages](/screenshots/legal-pages.png)

Your site starts with **Terms and Conditions**, **Privacy Policy**, **Acceptable Use Policy** and **Terms of Use**, at `/legal/` followed by the page name. Read each one carefully before you go live. They are your legal documents, so ask your legal adviser if you are unsure.

## Use the media library

Open **Media** at the top of the admin menu. It holds the pictures you have uploaded, and you can choose from it wherever the admin asks for a picture.

![The Media library](/screenshots/media-library.png)

## What your customers will see

Your wording on your public pages, your FAQs, your legal pages and your pictures.

## Tips

- Re-read your wording after you change how you run things, such as your refund approach.
- Keep the wording about complaints and contact up to date.

## Common questions

**Why did my wording stop following the wizard?**
Once you edit a piece, it is **Yours**. Use **Reset to automatic** to go back.

**Can I add a new legal page?**
You can edit the pages listed. For a new page, make one in the builder.
