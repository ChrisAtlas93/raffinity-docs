# Choose your template, colours and logo

Your site's look is set in one place. You choose a template for the layout, then your own colours, fonts and logo on top of it. This guide shows how. It is for admins only.

## Before you start

- Have your logo files ready, a version for light backgrounds and a version for dark.
- Know your brand colours. You can type them as a hex code, for example #15242c.

## Open the settings

Open **Website**, then **Branding & Appearance**. The page is called **Theme Branding**. Click **Save Branding** when you finish.

## Choose a template

Under **Website template**, choose the **Template**: **Default**, **Neon**, **Luxury**, **Performance** or **Pop**. Switching keeps all your colours and settings. Your colours, fonts, corners and shadows apply to every template.

![The Website template section](/screenshots/theme-template.png)

Also choose the **Ticket selector**, which is how customers choose how many tickets to buy: **Plus and minus buttons**, or **Slider (with plus and minus underneath)**. It works with every template.

### Mix and match

**Mix and match** lets you use a part of a different template, for example the competition card from one template and everything else from another. Parts left on "Same as the site template" follow your main choice.

## Colour mode and colours

- **Site colour mode.** **Light** or **Dark**. This is the mode every visitor sees. It does not follow their device.
- **Colours - Light** and **Colours - Dark.** Each set has colours for the page background, cards, text, borders, your primary and accent colours, the text that sits on them, and success and danger colours. Type a hex code, or a CSS colour function such as `oklch(55% 0.15 145)`.

![The colour settings](/screenshots/theme-colours.png)

## Fonts, corners and layout

- **Typography.** **Heading Font**, **Body Font** and **Font Scale**.
- **Corner radius.** From small to pill.
- **Shadows.**
- **Buttons & layout.** **Primary button fill**, **Secondary button fill** and **Site Width**.

## Add your logo and icons

Under **Logo & Favicon**:

- **Logo Light (for dark backgrounds).**
- **Logo Dark (for light backgrounds).**
- **Logo Mobile (square variant, optional).**
- **Favicon (browser tab).**
- **App icon (home screen).**
- **Logo Height in Nav (px).**

![The logo settings](/screenshots/theme-logo.png)

Your emails have their own logo setting. See [Brand your emails and set up sending](/emails/brand-and-send-emails).

## Change the look from the builder

The **Site** tab in the site builder has the main choices too: template, light and dark colours with a **Main colour** and **Accent colour**, **Heading font** and **Body font**, **Site width** and how customers choose tickets. It has no draft. **Apply to the live site** puts the change live at once. See [Edit your pages with the site builder](/your-website/edit-pages-in-the-builder).

## Update your trust bar

The trust bar is the strip of figures on your home page.

1. Open **Website**, then **Trust Bar**.
2. For each item, set a **Label** and a **Value**. The **ID** is not shown to visitors.
3. Switch on **Computed live** for an item that should show a live figure. The winners figure and the paid-out figure are worked out for you.
4. Use **Add item**, and drag to reorder.
5. Click **Save**.

![The Trust Bar screen](/screenshots/trust-bar.png)

If you also fill in **Figures** in the builder's Trust bar section, that replaces these items.

## What your customers will see

Your site in your template, colours, fonts and logo. Changes you save go live straight away.

## Tips

- Check your colours in both light and dark if you use both.
- Look at the site on a phone after any change.
- Use a logo with a transparent background.

## Common questions

**Can I use my own fonts?**
You can choose from the fonts offered in the font lists.

**Will switching template lose my colours?**
No. Switching keeps all your colours and settings.

**Where is custom code?**
Custom code is managed by Raffinity.
