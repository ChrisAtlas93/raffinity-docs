# Run Collect the Sets

Collect the Sets turns your instant wins into collectable cards. You group some of them into a set, such as "Elemental Starters". A customer who collects every card in a set wins a bonus prize. It works well with a mini game that reveals the cards.

## Before you start

- Check that **Collect the sets** is switched on. See [Switch features on and off](/getting-started/switch-features-on-and-off).
- Save the competition with its instant wins first. The form says: "Save the competition first, then come back to add instant wins to this set."
- Decide the sets, which instant wins belong in each, and the bonus prize.

## Add a set

1. Open the competition and click the **Games & engagement** tab.
2. In the **Collect the Sets** section, switch on **Enable Collect the Sets**.
3. Click **Add Set** and fill in:
   - **Set Name.** Required.
   - **Sort Order.**
   - **Prize Name.** Required. For example "£10 Site Credit".
   - **Prize Value.** Required.
   - **Auto-award as Site Credit.** Switch on to credit the prize value to the winner's wallet automatically.
   - **Win Limit.** Leave it blank for unlimited completions.
   - **Active.**
   - **Description** and **Set Image.**
4. Under **Instant Wins in this Set**, click **Add Instant Win**. For each card choose the **Instant Win**, the **Card rarity** and the **Display Order**.
5. Click **Save changes**.

![The Collect the Sets section](/screenshots/collect-sets-section.png)

### Card rarity

Each card is **Standard**, **Rare** or **Legendary**. If you leave it as automatic, the rarity follows the prize value. You set the dividing lines in **Settings**, then **Business & Site Settings**, in the section **Prize Pack Reveal**: **Rare from (£)** and **Legendary from (£)**.

## Check who has completed a set

On the competition's edit page, below the form, the **Set Completions** list shows who completed a set. It is a record only.

## If the prize is not Site Credit

If you switched on **Auto-award as Site Credit**, the prize is paid for you. If the bonus prize is something else, such as a physical item, there is no button in the admin to mark it as sent. Keep your own record of those and arrange delivery with the winner.

## What your customers will see

On the competition page, a panel shows the sets and which cards they have collected. When they buy tickets, cards can be revealed as a pack, which is especially fun with a mini game. Completing a set shows a completed-set message and the bonus prize.

## Tips

- Mix rarities so there is something special to hunt for.
- Use **Win Limit** if you only want a few bonus prizes to be given out.

## Common questions

**Does Duplicate copy my sets?**
No. Sets are not copied when you duplicate a competition. Add them again to the copy.
