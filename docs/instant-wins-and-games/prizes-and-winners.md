# Send prizes and show winners

Two jobs follow an instant win. You send any physical prizes, and you decide how winners are shown on your site. This guide covers both.

## Before you start

- **Prize Claims** can be used by moderators by default. The winner display settings are for admins.
- You need the customer's delivery address if you are posting a prize. It is on the claim.

## Send physical prizes

1. Open **Competitions**, then **Prize Claims**. The page itself is titled "Instant Win Claims".
2. Use the tabs to narrow the list:
   - **Physical prize.** Prizes that must be sent. It shows how many are ready.
   - **Cash** and **Site credit.** Prizes paid instantly.
   - **Non claimable.** Wins with nothing to pay.
   - **All.**
3. Each row shows the **Prize**, the **Winner**, the **Status**, the **Order** and when it was **Won**.

![The Prize Claims list](/screenshots/prize-claims.png)

### The statuses

- **Awaiting claim.** The winner has not yet chosen what they want. There is nothing for you to do.
- **Ready to send.** The winner has claimed a physical prize. Post it, then mark it fulfilled.
- **Fulfilled.** You have sent it.
- **Paid.** A Cash Wallet or Site Credit prize that was paid.
- **Payment failed.** A paid prize that could not be paid. Check the claim.
- **Win only.** Nothing to pay.

### Mark a prize as sent

1. Click the row to open the claim, or use **Quick look** for a summary.
2. When the status is **Ready to send**, post the prize.
3. Click **Mark fulfilled**. The winner is emailed to say it is on its way.

You can also tick several claims and use the bulk action **Mark as fulfilled**.

A claim shows the prize, what the winner chose, the winner's details, the competition, the winning ticket and a progress record of won, claimed and fulfilled. The **Fulfilment** section also has a **Notes** box for your own records.

### Delete a claim

Deleting a claim is rarely right. The winner is not told, and a payment already made is not taken back.

## Show winners on your site

1. Open **Settings**, then **Business Rules**, then the **Instant Win Settings** tab.
2. Click **Edit** on the row.
3. Under **Display**:
   - **Grid columns.** How many columns the instant prizes are laid out in, from 2 to 5.
   - **Show winner names publicly.** Switch off to hide names.
   - **Winner name format.** Choose **Full name**, **Full name, last initial** (for example John S.) or **First name only**.
4. Under **Live Winners Feed**, which controls the public feed on your home page:
   - **Enable live winners feed.**
   - **Feed title** and **Feed subtitle.**
   - **Minimum prize value to show (£).** Only wins at or above this amount appear. Set it to 0 to show all wins.
   - **Number of recent wins to display.**
5. Click **Save changes**.

![The winner display settings](/screenshots/winner-display-settings.png)

These name settings also decide how names appear for main draw winners and on leaderboards, as well as for instant wins.

## What your customers will see

- Winners see their prize in their account, and a physical prize shows what they need to do.
- Your home page shows the live winners feed, if you switch it on, with the names set above.
- Customers receive an email when a physical prize is marked as sent.

## Tips

- Do not let physical prizes sit as **Ready to send**. Customers who have won are waiting.
- Use the **Minimum prize value to show** setting to keep small wins out of the feed.
- Choose a name format you are comfortable with. Names are shown publicly.

## Common questions

**Why is the winner not claiming?**
The prize stays **Awaiting claim** until they choose. Nothing is needed from you until then.

**Can I see only the claims waiting for me?**
Yes. Use the **Physical prize** tab, which shows how many are ready to send. The **Awaiting claim only** filter shows the ones still waiting on the winner.

**Can I export the list?**
Admins can use **Export CSV**. The export includes delivery addresses, so handle it with care.
