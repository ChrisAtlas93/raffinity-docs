# Add a jackpot

A jackpot is one hidden winning ticket with a prize pot that grows as customers buy tickets. Whoever's order holds the winning ticket wins the whole pot. It is shown to customers like your other instant wins, with a pot that keeps climbing.

## Before you start

- Save the competition first. A jackpot is added from the **Instant wins** tab of a saved competition, not while you create it.
- A competition can only have one jackpot.
- Decide how the pot should grow, and whether to start it empty or seed it.

## Add the jackpot

1. Open the competition and click the **Instant wins** tab.
2. Click **Add Jackpot** above the prizes table.
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Name.** What customers see. It starts as "Jackpot".
   - **How the pot grows.** Choose **A percentage of the real money paid** or **A fixed amount for every ticket bought**.
   - **Percentage of real money paid**, or the amount per ticket, depending on your choice.
   - **Starting pot.** Leave it at £0 to start empty, or seed it. For example, with the pot left unwon on a previous competition.
   - **Ceiling (optional).** The most the pot can ever reach. Leave it empty for no ceiling.
   - **Pay the winner straight into their Cash Wallet.** See [How the winner is paid](#how-the-winner-is-paid).
4. Click **Submit**.

A random unsold ticket is picked as the hidden winner when you add the jackpot.

## What makes the pot grow

Only real money counts: card or gateway payments. Tickets paid for with Site Credit add nothing to the pot.

## How the winner is paid

- **Switch on** "Pay the winner straight into their Cash Wallet", and the pot is paid automatically.
- **Leave it off**, and the winner claims the jackpot from their account and you pay it by hand.

## Change a jackpot

On the prize's row, click **Edit jackpot**. Once sales start, the rules for how the pot grows are locked. You can still change the ceiling, but it is shown to customers, so change it with care.

## What your customers will see

A jackpot card on the competition page with a pot that grows as tickets sell.

## Tips

- Seeding the pot with an unwon jackpot from an earlier competition is a good way to build excitement.
- Use a ceiling if you want to cap your risk.

## Common questions

**Can Amplify boost a jackpot?**
No. Amplify only boosts Cash Wallet and Site Credit prizes.
