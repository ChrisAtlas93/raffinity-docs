# Add a mini game

A mini game gives customers a fun way to reveal their instant win results, instead of seeing them at checkout. They might scratch a card, spin a wheel or fire a penalty. The result is decided before they play. The game only reveals it, so playing cannot change the outcome.

## Before you start

- Check that **Minigames** is switched on. See [Switch features on and off](/getting-started/switch-features-on-and-off).
- The competition needs instant wins. A mini game reveals them. See [Add instant wins to a competition](/instant-wins-and-games/instant-wins).
- You need to be an admin.

## Add a mini game to a competition

1. Open the competition and click the **Games & engagement** tab.
2. In the **Minigame** section, switch on **Enable minigame reveal**.
3. Choose the **Minigame type**:
   - Scratchcard
   - Slots
   - Spin the Wheel
   - Plinko
   - Darts
   - Flip the Tiles
   - Fishing
   - Balloon Pop
   - Treasure Dig
   - Penalty Kick
4. Switch on **Show "Try me now!" demo** if you want a free demo on the competition page. It starts off on every new competition. It is clearly labelled, no real prizes are won and nothing is saved.
5. Optionally choose a **Theme**. Leave it as **Built-in look** to use the standard art.
6. If you chose Flip the Tiles, choose the **Tile backs**: **Jade (green stone)** or **Your site colour**.
7. Click **Save changes**.

## Customise the game for one competition

Under **Minigame Customisation** you can set:

- **Accent colour.** Overrides your site's main colour for this game only. Leave it blank to use the site default.
- **Win message.** Shown when a customer wins. Leave it blank for the default "YOU WON!".
- **Loss message.** Shown when no prize is won.
- **Background image.** Displayed behind the game. It works well for themed competitions such as Christmas or Valentine's.
- **Brand icon / watermark.** Shown faintly on the game pieces and on the loading screen. Use your logo or any themed icon.

## Make your own theme

A theme gives a game your own art, colours and wording, and you can use it on any competition that uses the same game.

1. Open **Competitions**, then **Minigame Themes**.
2. Click **Download theme pack** and choose a game. The pack is made for that one game.
3. Make your new art, for example with an image AI tool.
4. Click **New theme**.
5. Fill in the **Theme** section: a **Name**, the **Game** and any **Notes**. A theme belongs to one game, because its art only fits that game.
6. Under **Colours**, set a **Main colour** and an **Accent colour (buttons, highlights)**, or leave them blank to use your site colours. A competition's own accent colour still wins over these.
7. Under **Wording**, change any text you want. Leave a box blank to keep the game's normal wording.
8. Click **Create**.
9. On the theme's page, upload your art one piece at a time, or use **Upload a ZIP of art** to add them all at once. **Preview** shows how it looks.

Then open a competition that uses that game, go to **Games & engagement**, and pick your theme in the **Theme** box.

![The Minigame Themes list](/screenshots/minigame-themes-list.png)

![The Create Minigame Theme form](/screenshots/minigame-theme-create.png)

On the theme's page you can also **Duplicate** a theme, **Download theme pack** again, or **Delete** it. A theme that a competition is using cannot be deleted.

## What your customers will see

After they buy tickets, customers play the game to reveal what they won. They can find their games in their account under their minigames. If you left the demo switched on, they can try a free demo on the competition page first.

## Tips

- Try each game yourself with the **Try me now!** demo before you pick one.
- Keep your win and loss messages short and friendly.
- Use the same theme on several competitions to build a recognisable look.

## Common questions

**Does the game change who wins?**
No. The result is set before the customer plays.

**Why can I not see my theme in the list?**
A theme only appears for the game it was made for. Check the **Game** on the theme.

**Why do the game names differ between screens?**
The competition form says "Spin the Wheel" and "Flip the Tiles". The theme form says "Prize Wheel" and "Tiles". They are the same games.
