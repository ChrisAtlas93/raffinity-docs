# Run a leaderboard and give bonus points

A leaderboard ranks customers on a competition by points they win from instant wins. These are separate from the loyalty points customers earn when they spend. It can run for the whole competition or reset every day, with Cash Wallet prizes for the top places. Bonus points are a separate setting that gives customers extra loyalty points on a competition.

## Before you start

- Check that **Leaderboards** is switched on. See [Switch features on and off](/getting-started/switch-features-on-and-off).
- Points come from the **names** of your instant wins. Name some of them with points, for example "+3 Points" or "-1 Point". See [Add instant wins to a competition](/instant-wins-and-games/instant-wins).
- Use the prize type **Win only (nothing to pay)** for points prizes, so nothing is paid for them.

A name is only read as points when the word **Point** or **Points** is in it. A name such as "£100 Cash" or "Save -10 on your next order" scores nothing.

## Add a leaderboard

1. Open the competition and click the **Games & engagement** tab.
2. In the **Instant Win Leaderboard** section, switch on **Enable leaderboard**.
3. Choose the **Mode**:
   - **All Time.** Scores add up for the whole competition.
   - **24 Hour Reset.** The board resets every day.
4. For 24 Hour Reset, set the **Daily reset time**. This is when the board resets and the previous day's results are frozen.
5. Decide **Allow negative scores**. When off, scores stop at 0. When on, minus prizes can take a score below zero.
6. Click **Save changes**.

## Pay prizes to the top places

This works with the 24 Hour Reset mode.

1. Switch on **Pay prizes at each reset**.
2. Under **Prize for each place**, set the **Cash Wallet prize** for 1st place.
3. Click **Add next place** for 2nd, 3rd and so on. The first row is 1st place, the second is 2nd.
4. Click **Save changes**.

At every daily reset, the Cash Wallet money is paid to the top places. If you allow negative scores, the top places win whatever their score, so first place on -8 still wins. Otherwise only players with a score above zero win.

## See who was paid

Open **Competitions**, then **Leaderboard Winners**. The page is titled "Leaderboard Prizes". It shows every prize paid at a reset: when it was **Paid**, the **Competition** and day, the **Place**, the **Player**, their **Score** and the **Prize**. By default it shows the last 7 days. Change that under **Filters**.

![The Leaderboard Winners list](/screenshots/leaderboard-winners.png)

## Give bonus points on a competition

1. On the same **Games & engagement** tab, find the **Bonus points** section.
2. Type a **Bonus Points Multiplier**. For example, 2 means double loyalty points on this competition.
3. Click **Save changes**.

![The Bonus points section](/screenshots/bonus-points-section.png)

This only affects customers' loyalty points, the points they earn as they spend and can swap for Site Credit. It does not change leaderboard scores. The **Points** feature needs to be switched on. See [Switch features on and off](/getting-started/switch-features-on-and-off).

## What your customers will see

The competition page shows the leaderboard, with a countdown to the next reset. Prizes paid at a reset land in their Cash Wallet. Names on the board follow your winner name settings. See [Send prizes and show winners](/instant-wins-and-games/prizes-and-winners).

## Tips

- Use a 24 Hour Reset board for competitions that run over several days. It keeps customers coming back daily.
- Only allow negative scores if you want minus prizes to be able to take a score below zero.
- Check **Leaderboard Winners** the day after your first reset to make sure the payments went through.

## Common questions

**Why do my points prizes score nothing?**
Check the prize name contains the word Point or Points with a number, such as "+3 Points".

**Do leaderboard prizes cost me money?**
Yes. Cash Wallet prizes are real money that customers can withdraw.

**Can I change the reset time later?**
Yes, but customers will notice, so tell them first.
