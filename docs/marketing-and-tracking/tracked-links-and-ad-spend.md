# Build tracked links and record ad spend

A tracked link records where a visitor came from. If you also tell your site what you spend on an advert, your reports can show what each sale cost you. This guide shows how to do both. It is for admins only.

## Before you start

- Decide your naming. Use the same words every time, in lower case with no spaces. Spaces become underscores. This is what makes your reports group properly.
- Connect the platforms you advertise on. See [Count visits and connect ad platforms](/marketing-and-tracking/connect-tracking).

## Set up an ad campaign

An ad campaign ties the money you spend to the visits and sales it brings.

1. Open **Marketing**, then **Ads & Tracking**, then the **Ad Campaigns & Spend** tab.
2. Click **New ad campaign**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name.** Only you see this, for example "Tesla Y - Prospecting".
   - **Source.** Where the advert runs, for example meta, tiktok, google or snapchat.
   - **Medium.** The kind of advert, for example paid_social or cpc.
   - **Campaign name in the link.** The campaign name used in the link. Lower case, no spaces.
   - **Status.** **Active**, **Paused** or **Archived**.
   - **Notes.**
4. Click **Create**.

The source, medium and campaign must match the link used in the advert exactly. The Tracked Link Builder makes the link for you.

![The Create Ad Campaign form](/screenshots/ad-campaign-create.png)

## Build a tracked link

1. Open the **Tracked Links** tab. The page is called **Tracked Link Builder**.
2. Under **Where the link goes**, choose the **Page**. Choose **Another page** to type one.
3. Under **What brought the visitor**, either pick **Fill in from an ad campaign**, or choose **None: type my own** and fill in:
   - **Source**, **Medium** and **Campaign.** Required.
   - **Content (optional).** Tells apart two adverts in the same campaign.
   - **Term (optional).** A search word, for search adverts.
   - **Promo code (optional).** The code is remembered for the visitor for 7 days and applied for them, if the code allows it.
4. Your tracked link appears under **Your tracked link**. Copy it and use it in your advert, email or post.

![The Tracked Link Builder](/screenshots/tracked-link-builder.png)

## Record what you spend

Open an ad campaign and use the spend list on its page.

- **Add spend for a period.** Enter the spend each day, in pounds and **excluding VAT**.
- **Import spend from CSV.** Upload a file of spend from your ad platform, using the button on the **Ad Campaigns** list.

![The Ad Campaigns list](/screenshots/ad-campaigns-list.png)

## See the results

Open **Reports** and go to the marketing report. It shows visits, sales, your ad spend, and the cost of each sale and the return on your adverts, by source and campaign. See [Reports](/reports/).

## What your customers will see

Nothing different. The link takes them to the page you chose. If you added a promo code, it is applied for them.

## Tips

- Create the campaign before you run the advert, so spend and sales line up from the start.
- Use one naming pattern across all your adverts.
- Enter your spend regularly. A report is only as good as the spend you give it.

## Common questions

**Why do my reports not show cost per sale?**
The source, medium and campaign on the ad campaign must match the link exactly, and spend must be entered for the same period.

**Can I change a link later?**
A link already published keeps working. Build a new one if you want different tracking.
