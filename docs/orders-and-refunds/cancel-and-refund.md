# Cancel and refund an order

There are four ways to put right an order, and the right one depends on what you want to happen to the customer's money and tickets. This guide helps you choose, and takes you through each.

## Before you start

- Admins can use all of these. A moderator needs the matching switch, and all of them are off by default. The switches are in **Settings**, then **Business & Site Settings**, under **What moderators can do**.
- Every action asks for a **Reason**. It is for your records. The customer is not shown it.
- Cancelling and refunding cannot be undone.

## Which one should I use?

| Action | The money | The tickets | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cancel & refund** | Refunded | Go back into the competition | The customer should not have entered at all. |
| **Cancel, no refund** | Stays paid | Go back into the competition | An order broke your rules, and you are keeping the money. |
| **Refund, keep entries** | Refunded | Stay in the draw | The customer should keep their chance, for example after a payment problem. |
| **Goodwill credit** | A credit is added | Stay as they are | You want to compensate a customer. |

After a competition has been drawn, only **Refund, keep entries** and **Goodwill credit** are available.

## Find the actions

Open the order and click **Actions** at the top right. The same actions are in the **More actions** menu on a row of the **Orders** list.

![The order Actions menu](/screenshots/order-actions-menu.png)

## Cancel and refund an order

1. Click **Actions**, then **Cancel & refund**.
2. Type a **Reason**.
3. Click **Cancel and refund**.

![The Cancel and refund this order message](/screenshots/order-cancel-refund.png)

What happens:

- **The money goes back** in this order: Site Credit back to Site Credit, card money back to the card where a card provider is connected, and anything left to the Cash Wallet.
- **Cashback, points and any referral reward** earned on the order are taken back. Instant-win payouts are reversed.
- **The tickets** return to the competition, and instant win numbers become winnable again.
- **A promo code** used on the order is given back, so a one-use code can be used again.
- **The customer is emailed** to say the order was cancelled and refunded.

It is refused if the order is still being given its tickets, if a draw has already used its tickets, if the competition has been drawn, or while a provably fair draw is under way.

Loyalty milestone rewards are not taken back.

## Cancel with no refund

1. Click **Actions**, then **Cancel, no refund**.
2. Type a **Reason**, and confirm.

The tickets go back and rewards are taken back, but the money stays paid and still counts as revenue. The customer is emailed. You can still use **Cancel & refund** on the order later.

## Refund but keep entries

1. Click **Actions**, then **Refund, keep entries**.
2. Type a **Reason**, and confirm.

The money is refunded, but the tickets stay in the draw, and any instant win already won is kept. Rewards are taken back with the money. A promo code use is not given back. The customer is emailed. This is the only refund once a competition has been drawn.

## Give a goodwill credit

1. Click **Actions**, then **Goodwill credit**.
2. Enter the **Amount** in pounds, and a **Reason**.
3. Confirm.

The credit goes into the customer's Cash Wallet, so they can withdraw it. The total of goodwill credits on one order cannot be more than the order cost. The customer gets the "wallet credited" email.

## Cancel and refund many orders

1. In the **Orders** list, tick the orders.
2. Use the bulk action **Cancel & refund selected**.
3. Type a **Reason** and type CANCEL to confirm.

You can do up to 50 at a time. Orders that cannot be cancelled are skipped.

## If a customer's bank takes the money back

That is a chargeback, not a refund. See [Deal with a chargeback](/payments/chargebacks-and-held-credit).

## What your customers will see

An email telling them what happened, and the change in their account: a refund in their wallet or on their card, and the order marked cancelled or refunded. Card refunds can take a few working days to appear.

## Tips

- Write a clear reason. It helps whoever looks at the order later.
- Use **Refund, keep entries** rather than cancelling when the customer did nothing wrong.
- Check **Card refunds** after refunding a card order. See [Check card payments and refunds](/payments/check-card-payments).

## Common questions

**Can I refund part of an order?**
There is no partial refund. For part of the amount, use **Goodwill credit**.

**Why did the refund go to a wallet, not the card?**
Card money only goes back to the card when a card provider is connected and the refund can be sent. Otherwise it goes to the Cash Wallet.

**What if I cancelled by mistake?**
Cancelling cannot be undone. Create a new order for the customer with **Ticket Allocation**.
