# Offers and promotions

Ways to encourage sales. The price offers are set on the competition itself. Promo codes and popups live under **Marketing** in your admin.

## Price offers and boosts

- [Set up sale, Early Bird and Multibuy prices](/offers-and-promotions/sale-early-bird-multibuy). Three ways to lower a ticket price, and how they work together.
- [Boost instant wins with Amplify](/offers-and-promotions/amplify). Make cash and Site Credit prizes pay more for a while.

## Codes and popups

- [Create promo codes](/offers-and-promotions/promo-codes). Percentage off, amount off, free tickets and automatic offers.
- [Show a popup](/offers-and-promotions/popups). Promote a competition, an offer or your own message.

The first pass at these prices is in [Create a competition](/competitions/create-a-competition#add-offers-to-the-price).
