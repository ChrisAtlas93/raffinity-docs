# Show a popup

A popup is a message on your site that promotes a competition, an offer or your own news. You control who sees it, on which pages, when, and how often. A visitor sees one popup at a time, and never the same one twice in a visit.

## Before you start

- You need to be an admin. A moderator needs the switch for creating and editing popups, which is off by default.
- Have your wording ready. For a competition popup, make sure the competition is **Active**.

## See your popups

Open **Marketing**, then **Popups**. Each row shows the **Name**, its type and layout, its **Status**, whether it is **On**, and its **Views**, **Clicks** and **Click rate**. Drag the rows to set priority. When more than one could show, the top one wins.

![The Popups list](/screenshots/popups-list.png)

## Create a popup

1. Click **Create popup**.
2. Under **Popup**, fill in:
   - **Name (only you see this).** Required.
   - **Type.** **Competition**, **Promo** or **Custom**.
   - **Layout.** **Centred modal**, **Corner card** or **Bar**. On phones the corner card sits along the bottom.
   - **On.** Off keeps it ready without showing it.
3. Fill in the section for your type:
   - **Competition.** Pick the **Competition**. The picture, tickets left, price and offers come from the competition itself and stay up to date. You can set the **Heading**, **Subtitle**, **Button text** and a **Popup image (16:9, optional)**.
   - **Promo.** Choose what it is for: a promo code, Early Bird, Multibuy or Amplify. Then set the headline and where the button goes.
   - **Custom.** Write your own **Title** and **Message**, choose a style (standard, announcement or urgent) and add up to two buttons.
4. Optionally add a **Countdown**. Under **Counts down to** choose **No countdown**, **When the competition ends**, **When the Early Bird price ends**, **When the sale ends** or **A time I choose**. The popup hides itself when it reaches zero.
5. Under **When and where**, set:
   - **Show after (seconds).** It waits for the cookie choice first.
   - **Wait after it is closed or clicked (hours).** 0 means no wait.
   - **Most times per visit.** A visit is one browser tab session.
   - **Show after this many pages viewed.** 0 shows it on the first page.
   - **Show up to (times in total).** Per visitor, in the browser they use. Blank means no limit.
   - **Starts** and **Stops** (London time).
   - **Shown to.** **Everyone**, **Signed in customers** or **Signed out visitors**.
   - **Pages.** **All pages**, **Homepage only** or **Specific competitions**.
6. Check the **Preview**. Switch between **Phone** and **Desktop**.
7. Click **Create**.

![The Create Popup form](/screenshots/popup-create.png)

A popup is never shown on checkout, payment, sign in or account set-up pages.

## Start a popup from a competition

On a competition's edit page, **Create popup** starts a popup for that competition. The button only shows while the competition is **Active**.

## Find out why a popup is not showing

In the list, use **Why isn't this showing?** beside a popup. It helps you find what is stopping it.

## What your customers will see

A popup in the layout you chose, in your theme's colours and fonts, after they have made their cookie choice. They can close it, and it will not return in the same visit.

## Tips

- Use a countdown for real deadlines only.
- Keep the message short, with one clear button.
- Check the click rate after a few days. A popup nobody clicks may need new wording.

## Common questions

**Why did I not see my own popup?**
Popups are counted per browser, and a visitor sees each one once per visit. Use **Why isn't this showing?**, or open your site in a private window.

**Can I show two popups at once?**
No. Only the one highest in the list shows, and a visitor sees one at a time.
