# Boost instant wins with Amplify

Amplify makes chosen cash and Site Credit instant wins pay more for a limited time. It suits a slow evening or the last hours before a draw. Customers see that prizes are boosted, and the odds of winning do not change.

## Before you start

- The competition must have instant wins. See [Add instant wins to a competition](/instant-wins-and-games/instant-wins).
- Only active **Cash Wallet** and **Site Credit** prizes with a value can be amplified. Physical prizes, win-only prizes and jackpots cannot.
- Amplify is set up on a saved competition. You need to be an admin.

## Create an Amplify

1. Open the competition and click the **Instant wins** tab.
2. Click the **Amplify** sub-tab, next to **Prizes**.
3. Click **New Amplify**.
4. Fill in the form:
   - **Name customers see.** Shown on the competition page and card. It cannot contain "Jackpot".
   - **Internal label (optional).** For you only, to tell Amplifies apart in the list.
   - **Prizes to amplify.** Tick one or more prizes. One prize group covers all of its numbers.
   - **How much more.** Choose **Multiply the prize** and set the **Multiplier** (1.25, 1.5, 2 and 3 are common), or **Add a fixed extra amount to each prize**.
   - **When it starts.** See [Choose when it starts](#choose-when-it-starts).
   - **Runs for.** Choose 1 hour, 3 hours, 6 hours, 24 hours, or **Another length** and type the hours.
5. Optionally set **Stops early if** limits:
   - **Extra paid reaches.** The amount of extra money, in pounds. Once the next win would pass it, the Amplify ends and every win after that pays its normal value.
   - **Tickets sold during it reach.** Counted from paid orders, so it can run slightly over.
6. Read the **Check** box. It shows what the Amplify could cost: the most extra you would pay if every unclaimed amplified prize is won.
7. Click **Submit**.

![The New Amplify form](/screenshots/amplify-new.png)

An Amplify always ends when its time is up. The stop limits are extra ways to cap what it costs.

### Choose when it starts

- **At a time I choose (or as soon as I save).** Set **Starts at**, or leave it blank to start straight away. Customers are not told about it before it starts.
- **Automatically, when a condition is met.** Set **Hours before the competition closes**, **Or when this % is sold**, or both. Switch on **Both must be true** if both conditions must be met. With it off, either one starts the Amplify.

## Manage Amplifies

The Amplify sub-tab lists each one with its status, what it pays, when it runs, the extra paid so far and the results.

![The Amplify tab](/screenshots/amplify-tab.png)

- **End now** stops a running Amplify. Wins from then on pay their normal value, and wins already amplified keep their extra.
- **Cancel** removes one that has not started.
- **Edit** changes one. While it is running, you can only change it in the customer's favour, and the prize values are locked.

## What your customers will see

A chip on the prize cards, and a banner with a countdown above the instant wins, showing the old and new values. Mini games do not look different while an Amplify runs.

## Tips

- Use the cost shown in the **Check** box to decide how big to go.
- Add an **Extra paid reaches** limit if you want a firm ceiling on cost.

## Common questions

**Does Amplify change the odds?**
No. Only the amount paid on a win changes.

**Does Duplicate copy my Amplify?**
No. Add it again to the copy.

**Can I amplify a physical prize?**
No. Only Cash Wallet and Site Credit prizes can be amplified.
